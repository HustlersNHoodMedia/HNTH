Yandy Smith and Mendeecees had their wedding on live TV in 2015, with the dress, the vows and the whole family watching, and they even renewed those vows years later, but they never signed the marriage license.

Yandy explained why on the Social Proof Podcast. When they had the wedding, Mendeecees was waiting to be sentenced on federal charges, and she knew restitution and child support could come with him. She grew up in the Grant Houses in New York and worked her way through Howard, Violator and Atlantic Records, and she wasn't putting what she built on the line.

"I did not want to be in business with the government and him," she said. "I wanted to be in business with him."

They used medical proxies and powers of attorney instead, so when they split after more than 20 years together, there was no divorce to file.
