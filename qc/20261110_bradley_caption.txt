Sunday afternoon in Shreveport, Sherman Bradley was driving with his 11-year-old son S.J. when the 18-wheeler in front of them leaned into a curve on Jefferson Paige Road and went over into the ditch.

Sherman didn't keep driving. He pulled over, climbed up the side of that truck with another man who stopped to help, and they got the driver out of the cab. No serious injuries.

S.J. grabbed the phone the second his dad got on top of the truck and recorded the whole thing. He wanted proof: "My dad is a hero! I wanted to record to show the moment my dad was a great, awesome hero!"

And yes, they still made it back in time for the Saints kickoff.
