Za'Darius Smith got the call from his mother's doctor in the middle of last season. She didn't have long. He told the Eagles and they would not let him go, so he retired. It was the only way out he had.

Sharon Smith died in February. His grandmother died after her, then his father.

He started working out again this summer and signed with Atlanta, the team his mother always wanted for him because it was the closest one to home. Home is Greenville, Alabama, where she worked 22 years as a correctional officer and where he bought her a house the day he told her she could stop working.

Sunday he plays his first home game. A Greyhound is pulling up at Harrison Street Baptist Church to bring 45 people from her congregation to the stadium, with the tickets, the shirts and the food on him, and an envelope for every one of them.

"It's something my mom always wanted."
