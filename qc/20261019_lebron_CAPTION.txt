In 1993, LeBron James was in fourth grade and he missed 83 days of school. Between the ages of five and eight, he and his mama Gloria had moved about a dozen times around Akron.

Then his youth football coach, Frank Walker, and his wife Pam opened their home to him. He lived with the Walkers for about a year and a half, and the next school year he didn't miss a single day.

"That following year I met the Walker family, and they had a support system."

In 2018 he opened the I Promise School in Akron, a public school for kids who were falling behind. The first students were third and fourth graders.

"I believe that's where it all kind of starts."
