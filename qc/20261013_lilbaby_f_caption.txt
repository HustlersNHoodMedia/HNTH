She had her whole art collection lined up on the grass by the road. She wasn't selling it for fun. She was getting evicted the next day.

Her ex had walked out, left her with $97,000 in debt and took her car with him. She told the camera the only thing she had left to hold on to was God.

Then a stranger mentioned his mom's birthday was coming up, and she tried to hand him a painting for free. "Please have it for your mom. From another mom. Just think of me when you pray."

That stranger was Zach Dereniowski, the creator behind @mdmotivator. A few minutes later Lil Baby walked over, blindfolded her and took her by the arm. When the blindfold came off there was a brand new car with the insurance paid, and then the last surprise. A full year of rent. She's not getting evicted.

Before they left, she was still trying to give them a painting.
