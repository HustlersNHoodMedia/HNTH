Ms. Pat was 19 when she met Garrett. She was selling drugs and forging checks. He worked at General Motors and had never gotten so much as a speeding ticket. She told Shannon Sharpe she thought he was lame.

He's the one who got her out. He put an apartment in his name so she could walk away from that life, and she worked odd jobs until the comedy took off. He helped raise her two oldest and her sister's four. Six kids that weren't his.

The ring on his finger is from back then. Ms. Pat says she paid for it with a forged check.

Now she has The Ms. Pat Show, four Emmy nominations and a new book out this week, and she retired him from General Motors. After 33 years she wants to buy him a new ring. He won't take the old one off.

"You have always been my ride or die," she wrote to him. "Words can't describe the husband, father, step daddy, uncle, and mentor you have been to our family."
