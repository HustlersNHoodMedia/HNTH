Blacc Sam just opened the newest Marathon Burger inside LAX, in Terminal 1.

He said that's the same terminal he and Nipsey used to fly out of every time they left LA.

He didn't just cut a ribbon. He was back in the kitchen in a white tee and gloves, cooking right next to his staff.

Nip started the Marathon brand in 2010. His big brother has kept it moving with Marathon Burger in Venice, Canoga Park, Long Beach and West Hollywood, and now the airport has its own breakfast menu you can't get at the other spots.

He also said Jay-Z has done more for the family and the business than people know.

The marathon continues.
