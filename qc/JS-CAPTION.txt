Johan Sandoval batted .369 last season for Savannah State. He started all 47 games, drove in 51 runs and stole 21 bases.

In June he was named MVP of the HBCU All-Star Game at Truist Park in Atlanta. He went 2-for-2 with an RBI double, scored a run and stole a base while his side won it 6-2.

On July 3 he was driving to the gym at seven in the morning in Clifton Park, New York. A truck pulled in front of his car. ICE took him into custody.

He spent almost two weeks in there. He slept on the floor and he didn't know why he'd been picked up until his lawyer explained it to him. The dispute is over his student record, and his attorney says he was enrolled full time the whole way through, going to class and playing ball. He just couldn't update the federal system himself.

A judge granted him bond on July 15. Twenty thousand dollars and an ankle monitor.

"The ankle bracelet is hard because a lot of people judge you. They start saying out loud that you're a criminal, that you're a bad person."

He's still trying to get back to Savannah State.
