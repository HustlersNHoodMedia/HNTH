Rev. Larry Hopkins opened St. Rest #2 Country Kitchen in Chatham in 1972. Smothered pork chops, oxtails, collard greens, three days a week, a line around the corner. Michael Jordan has eaten there.

In early 2021 he asked his daughter Daniella to take it over. She said no at first. Then he told her something she still repeats. Baby girl, what you're looking for way over yonder, it's right here in your hands.

She said yes in February. In April he died in her arms. The day after the funeral she was in foreclosure court, holding $600,000 of inherited debt with the bank ready to take the building.

She and her husband John, a retired Marine, sold their house and cashed out his retirement together. They were still $25,000 short with two days left. A grant she had applied for had already been denied. She checked the account anyway and there was $35,000 in it. Somebody had pulled the application back out and approved it.

The debt was paid off within a year. Her mother Sophia, 77, still worked the floor as lead waitress.
