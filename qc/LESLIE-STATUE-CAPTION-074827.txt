Lisa Leslie is from Inglewood. Morningside High. In 1990 she scored 101 points in one half and the other team refused to come out for the second.

She played all twelve of her WNBA seasons for the Sparks. Three MVPs, two championships, four Olympic golds, and in 2002 she did what nobody in the league had done before her: she dunked in a game.

On Sunday they pulled the cover off an eleven foot bronze of that dunk outside Crypto.com Arena. She's standing out there now with Kobe, Magic, Shaq and Kareem. First woman on that plaza. First Sparks player, period.

She made them get the details right. Ribbons in the hair. Nails polished. Her family's names on the bracelets.

"I never wanted the boys or the men to think that I was a boy."
