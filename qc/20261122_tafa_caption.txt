Mawena Tafa, 24, was taping up flyers for Pickup Buddi, the after-school pickup service she started with another college student, when a man started following her down the street in Brooklyn. He kept telling her she was breaking the law. Then he called the police and reported her for vandalism.

She pulled out her phone and kept recording. "I'm just a startup, you know. It's just a small startup, just me and my co-founder, who is also a college student."

The video passed 9 million views. Parents and students started signing up. And the company he worked for put out a statement saying he is no longer employed there.

She called the whole thing a blessing.
