In 1994 Master P had one album out that sold 250,000 copies with no label behind it. Jimmy Iovine flew him to Interscope and put a check for a million dollars in front of him.

He read the contract. Seven years. He couldn't use his own name. They took the rights.

"I told him I was going to get something to eat. I left and never came back."

Iovine told him if he walked out he would never get a deal in that town again. His brother C-Murder was so mad they almost fought on the plane home. Master P told him: that man offered me a million dollars, so how much do you think I'm worth?

Then he did the part nobody talks about. He found out Michael Jackson had the best deal in music, paid Michael Jackson's lawyer $25,000 to sit down with him, and walked into Priority Records asking for distribution only.

No Limit kept 100% of its masters. At the peak it was doing over $100 million a year.
