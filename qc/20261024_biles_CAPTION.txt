Simone Biles and her little sister Adria were in foster care when their grandfather Ron Biles and his wife Nellie stepped up and adopted them both.

For as long as Simone can remember, Ron and Nellie have been mom and dad, and that's exactly what she calls them.

They raised the most decorated gymnast in history, and they've been in the stands cheering her on at the Olympics.
