Baron Davis's sister Lissa says this started almost 20 years ago, right after she had her son in 2008 and was dealing with a medical emergency.

According to her lawsuit, Baron told her to sell the house she owned in Northridge and promised to help her get another one. He kept the title in his name "for tax purposes," but she says he told her it would always be hers. She and her husband moved in and put their savings into fixing it up.

In January, she says, she found out from a real estate agent that Baron was selling it. Baron had never told her.

Now Baron is asking a judge to throw the case out. His lawyers say a promise about a house has to be in writing, that she lived there rent-free for nearly two decades, and that "a familial relationship of trust" didn't mean he had to tell her anything.

The judge hears it in February.
