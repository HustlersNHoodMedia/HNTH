Thursday morning Deneen Jordan and her 20 year old son were carrying grocery bags home from the Save A Lot in East Baltimore when marked and unmarked cars pulled up and officers came out with rifles pointed at him. They put him on the ground and cuffed him.

She pulled out her phone and did not stop talking. "No, no, no. You have the wrong son. You have the wrong child. This is my child." An officer told her he had an open warrant. She said he did not. She asked for badge numbers. She said she had his ID in her hand. "Lock me up instead. Take me. Don't take him. Take me."

They put him in the back of a city cruiser and drove off anyway.

Here is what came out after. The officers were Baltimore County detectives helping federal postal inspectors serve a warrant for mail theft out of Northern Virginia. The man they wanted has the same name as her son. That is it. Same name.

Once they figured it out he was released without charges. The county has opened an administrative review of whether the officers followed policy and Maryland law. The family has a lawyer.
