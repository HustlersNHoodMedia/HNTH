When Kevin Hart decided to go all in on stand-up, his mama Nancy told him she wasn't a dream killer. She would cover his rent for one year while he tried.

Early on, he fell a month behind and went to her for the money. She asked him one thing. "Have you been reading your Bible?"

A week later he came back and got the same question. Then another week, same question. When the eviction notice went up on his door, he showed it to her. "Talk to me when you read your Bible."

So he went home and opened it. "Six rent checks fell out. She put all my rent checks in the Bible."

He told that story to Oprah in 2014. Nancy passed in 2007, before most of the world knew her son's name.
