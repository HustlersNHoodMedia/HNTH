Tisha Campbell just told the story of how she almost wasn't Gina.

She had shot a pilot for another show and it got picked up for 13 episodes. Steady money, and she was still living with her mom. Martin only had a pilot. So when Martin Lawrence asked her to play the role, she told him no.

He wasn't having it. "Nah, nah, I'm coming over your house. Where do you live?" She told him she lived with her mom. He came anyway and told her he really wanted her for the part.

She knew it was a lot of money to walk away from. She did it anyway, and Martin and Gina became the couple a whole generation grew up on, five seasons from 1992 to 1997.

And it wasn't the first time he asked. At the Boomerang premiere he had already told her he wanted her to play his wife or his girlfriend.

They went through their own falling out years later and worked their way back to each other as friends.
