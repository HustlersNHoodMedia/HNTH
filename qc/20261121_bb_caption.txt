Joe Foster took his 2-year-old son Cole up to Big Bear for Halloween weekend. On the last night, a little after 2 a.m., he woke up to glass breaking and smoke. The stairs were already on fire.

He carried Cole out to the second-floor balcony and yelled for help. Nobody answered. Then a ball of fire came out of the cabin and caught him on his right side, while Cole stayed tucked in his left arm.

Joe, a Marine veteran, looked down at a patch of dirt about 20 feet below. He told his son he was sorry, threw him, and jumped right after him.

Cole was checked out and went home the same day without a scratch. Joe is still recovering from burns on his face, arm and leg, with months of therapy ahead.

"Looking at my burns ... to know he's 100%, it gets me choked up."
