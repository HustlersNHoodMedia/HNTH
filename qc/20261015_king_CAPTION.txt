King is 10 years old, and he spent more than six years in foster care waiting on a family.

All five of his brothers and sisters got adopted before him. He was the only one still waiting.

Back in May he told a news crew in North Texas exactly what he was praying for: a family that believes in God like he does. He even had his favorite scripture ready. "I can do all things through Christ who strengthens me."

Five months later, he's living with the Thomas family. He likes his new school, they already took him to the State Fair, and he'll be the couple's first child together, with two grown siblings looking out for him. The adoption becomes official in February.

"King has been a light in our home, and we enjoy having him around. We look forward to growing together."
