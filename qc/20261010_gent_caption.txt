Six young men from I Am A Gentleman, a mentoring program on Chicago's South Side, spent more than two months getting ready for the anniversary of the March on Washington. In August they had their bags packed and were on their way to the bus when a text came in at 2:53. The 4:30 bus wasn't coming. None of the buses were.

"I was heartbroken," said Jaren Johnson, 16, one of the six.

The program's director, Jermaine Anderson, spent the next six weeks making it right. Donors, the Chicago Police Department, Father Michael Pfleger and WVON all stepped in. This weekend, more than 50 young men got on a bus to DC.

Their first stop is Howard's homecoming. After that, the National Museum of African American History and Culture and Capitol Hill.
