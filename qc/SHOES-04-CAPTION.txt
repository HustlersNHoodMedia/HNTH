One pair of shoes. That's all it took to turn a living room into the happiest place on the internet.

He tore the wrapping off the box, saw what was inside, and lost it. Screaming, holding the box up, checking it again like it might not be real. By the end of the video the whole family is jumping around the living room with him, wrapping paper everywhere.

His sister Ashley filmed it and posted it so people could laugh along. Almost 8 million did. Back in May she watched the same little brother walk across a graduation stage, holding back tears watching him grow into the man he's supposed to be. Two months later she's the one laughing hardest in the room.

Some houses don't need much to be rich.
