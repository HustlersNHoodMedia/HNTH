Latasha Reed-Hopkins, everybody calls her Tootie, started cooking for money in 2023 because her family needed it. Catering out of Tallahassee, plate by plate, for three years. The whole time the goal was a door with her name on it.

In April she got it. Tootie's Cafe and Catering opened on John Knox Road. One month in, a letter from the property manager: the commercial lease was invalid and she was facing eviction. Her words: "I couldn't understand why everything was happening."

The people who had been eating her food did not let it end there. They helped her find a new building about 15 minutes up the road in Havana, a small town with no soul food spot until now. Green trim, OPEN sign, brunch on the window.

She reopened this week. Folks are driving in from Tallahassee, Cairo and Bainbridge. A couple drove up from Pensacola just to sit down and eat.

"I feel like everything happens for a reason. I had everyone rallying behind me."
