Michelle Williams left Illinois State at 20 because Destiny's Child called. Two decades of records, tours, Broadway, a Grammy. She never went back to finish.

Until now. At 47 she is a sophomore at Regent University, studying psychology.

She almost didn't post it. She says she didn't even tell family and friends, because of the one thought that shows up every time somebody tries to go back: what if you don't finish?

She posted it anyway, in the school shirt, from the library.

"I'm going to finish and I'm going to finish strong."
