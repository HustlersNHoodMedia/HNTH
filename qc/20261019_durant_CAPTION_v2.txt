Wanda Durant had her first son at 18. By 21 she was a single mom raising two boys in Prince George's County, Maryland.

They moved from apartment to apartment. When they finally got a place of their own, there was no bed and no furniture. "We just all sat in the living room and hugged each other because we thought we made it," Kevin remembered.

Some nights there wasn't enough food. She made sure her boys ate, and she went to bed without.

On May 6, 2014, her youngest accepted the NBA MVP in Oklahoma City. He thanked his teammates one by one, his coaches, his brother. Then he turned to her in the front row.

"You went to sleep hungry. You sacrificed for us. You the real MVP."
