Levi Johnson is 7 and was born with sickle cell. Pain, exhaustion, hospital stays. His parents, Brandon and Jillian, had made peace with it.

"We were prepared to just live life with sickle cell and just manage it as we had to. So, when this opportunity came about, we were just grateful," Brandon said.

Doctors at Children's Healthcare of Atlanta brought up a bone marrow transplant. They tested his brother Bryce, who is 8. A full sibling has a one in four chance of being a match. Bryce was a perfect one.

The family got split up while Levi recovered. They ended up back together in a transplant suite at the Ronald McDonald House in Brookhaven, where Levi can shut himself in when he needs to and come out and play when he doesn't.

He is still recovering. What he wants is to be cleared to go back to school and hang out with his best friend, who is Bryce.

"It's just been a whirlwind of take it a day at a time," Jillian said.
