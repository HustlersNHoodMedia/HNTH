In 2005, T.I.'s uncles put together about $200 and fed 100 families for Thanksgiving in their Atlanta neighborhood.

This week was year 21. Cars lined up outside Jackson Memorial Baptist Church, and T.I. was out there with his mama, his aunts, his uncles, his wife Tiny, his kids and his cousins, loading groceries for more than 2,000 families.

He says he keeps doing it because of a voice in his head that tells him it's something good and it should continue.

"Everyone is here to support the community that supported us first," he said.
