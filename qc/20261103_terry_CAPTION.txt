Scotesha Terry is a pediatric nurse in Jacksonville. On Halloween, a judge made her Daniel's mom.

Daniel is 13 and has level five cerebral palsy. He needs care around the clock. He weighed 29 pounds when he came into the children's hospital and stayed more than a year. Without a family, the plan was a nursing home hundreds of miles away.

Scotesha showed up to the first meeting with three pages of questions about his medical history. His case manager told the court nobody had ever come that prepared.

A couple weeks ago his insurance tried to cut his nursing hours down to 16 a day. She pushed back and got all 24 back.

She's a mom of four now, and Daniel has a little brother, 12-year-old Kingston, who says he's happy he's not the only boy in the house anymore.

"Don't look at the diagnosis. Look at the child. Every child deserves a home."
