Lil' Kim's debut album Hard Core came out on November 12, 1996. Thirty years to the day later, she's flipping the switch to light the Empire State Building pink.

Before any of that, she left home at 14 with nowhere to go. "There was a time when my mother and I were living out of the trunk of her car," she has said.

Biggie heard her rap on a Brooklyn street corner and put her in Junior M.A.F.I.A., the only woman in the group. Then he executive produced her first solo album. Hard Core went double platinum and became one of the defining albums for women in rap.

Next week she takes it home to Brooklyn with the Hard Core Symphony at Kings Theatre, her first show with a full orchestra. The November 19 date sold out in four days, so they added a second night.
