Adonis was born last October in Maple Grove, Minnesota, at 24 weeks and five days. One pound, eight ounces. His mama, Jasmin Wellington, went into labor way too early and nobody in that room was promising her anything.

"The first couple of days, he was really, really sick," his nurse said. "I remember going home and coming back and saying, I hope and pray he's still here."

He was on a breathing tube for months. He caught MRSA. He caught pneumonia. Every time, he came back.

Friday, after 334 days, they dressed him in a little blue cap and gown and walked him out of the NICU. Longest stay that hospital has ever had. His big brother was standing at the crib waiting on him.

"It's hard to describe a long NICU journey like this," Jasmin said. "I really don't know how I even survived."

He's home.
