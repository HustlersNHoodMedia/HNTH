Back in July, Hercy Miller said it out loud: if anybody ever made a movie about his father, Master P, the part had to be his. He knows the mannerisms, he knows the talk, and in his words nobody could be dad better than him.

Last week Universal gave him the answer. Deadline confirmed Hercy will play Master P in "Snoop," the Snoop Dogg biopic.

He grew up in the house where No Limit was built. Now the 24-year-old gets to put that on screen in his first feature film, and his pops has already posted how proud he is.

Hercy's reaction: "Like father, like son. I'M BOUT IT BOUT IT!"
