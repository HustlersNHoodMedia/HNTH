Kendrick Lamar was 8 years old in 1995, when 2Pac and Dr. Dre came to Compton to shoot the "California Love" video.

His dad put him up on his shoulders so he could see over the crowd. He still remembers the car. "I think it was a white Bentley," he said.

About 15 years later, Dre heard his music and brought him to his label, Aftermath. In 2012, good kid, m.A.A.d city came out with Dre's name on it.

When they finally met, Kendrick told him the story.

"Dre, I was one of them kids that was there."
