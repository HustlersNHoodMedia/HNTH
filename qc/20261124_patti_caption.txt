Patti LaBelle lost all three of her sisters to cancer, every one of them in their 40s.

Two boys used to live across the street from her family. When their mother passed, Patti and her husband Armstead adopted them both. One of those boys, Stanley, grew up and went to Harvard Law.

In 1989 her youngest sister Jackie passed from cancer too, and Patti adopted Jackie's two kids. Her niece Stayce still calls her "aunty mommy."

With her son Zuri, that made five.

After Jackie, she said: "Now, I don't wait to show people how much I love them. And I don't wait to help them. If I can do it now, I do it now."
