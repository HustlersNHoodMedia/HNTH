7815 South Shore Drive. That's the house. Donda West taught English at Chicago State for almost thirty years, ran the department, started a center for Black writers, and came home every night to a living room full of teenage boys making beats.

Rhymefest. GLC. Really Doe. Don C. Her son. They all say the same thing: she wasn't Kanye's mama, she was everybody's.

One night Kanye's car died downtown. She got out of bed at 4 in the morning and drove to get them. When the boys dropped out of college to chase music, she didn't lose it. She told him she was proud of him.

She passed in 2007. She was 58.

On Tuesday the city put her name on the corner of 78th and South Shore. Ye came home, stood with his old friends, and didn't take the mic. He held the sign up himself.

"This is not just a story about Donda West's son. This is a story about a woman who raised a community of boys."
