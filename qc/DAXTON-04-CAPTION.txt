Branson Davis was 17, two months into his senior year at Riverton High in Utah. He was enrolled in the school's aviation program, working toward his pilot's license, with a dream of one day flying fighter jets. He had been holding down a job since he was 15. In October, a motorcycle accident took his life.

In June, his class graduated. Students wore red bracelets in his honor. And when it came time for Branson's honorary diploma, his little brother Daxton put on the gown and crossed the stage carrying his brother's photo to accept it for him.

Their mom said her heart was broken that Branson wasn't there to take that walk himself, and overflowing with pride watching his little brother step up in his place.

Branson never got to take that walk. Daxton made sure it still happened.
