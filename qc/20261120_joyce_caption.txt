Ms. Joyce is 81 and a retired teacher. This summer she got a cancer diagnosis.

Her youngest daughter Lori bought tickets to see Chris Brown at SoFi Stadium. In her words, "She wanted something to look forward to."

During "Take You Down," the dancers brought Ms. Joyce up onstage in her fur coat. Chris walked right up to her and sang to her face to face, with the whole stadium watching.

She said she didn't have time to get nervous. She just went with it.

A couple of days later Chris posted the photos of the two of them and wrote two words under them: "LOVE HER."
