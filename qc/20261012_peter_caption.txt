Peter Mutabazi ran away from home at 10 and grew up on the streets. Today he's a single foster dad in Charlotte, and more than 48 kids have come through his door.

A few days ago a social worker called. A 4-year-old boy was at the hospital and needed a home right away.

Before he gave an answer, Peter sat his kids down at the kitchen counter and asked them. He told them the little man would probably be scared, so they'd have to share their toys and be gentle with him.

Every one of them said yes.

Then he went and got the room ready, down to a pair of little slippers next to the crib.

"We don't know how long we'll have him, but we said yes."
