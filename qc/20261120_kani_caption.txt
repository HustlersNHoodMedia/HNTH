Karl Kani was born Carl Williams and grew up in Brooklyn, watching his father, a tailor, sell matching linen suits around the neighborhood.

When he started making clothes himself, he wasn't sure a kid from the inner city could really pull off his own brand. So he named it after the question. Kani. Can I.

In his words, "it all started with one question: 'Can I?'"

He answered it. Tupac, Aaliyah and Biggie wore Karl Kani, and the name is still on clothes more than 35 years later.

This week he put the whole story in his memoir, "Karl Kani: A Life By Design."
