D'Amber Davis stopped at the same gas station in Central Georgia almost every day, and the cashier, Katelyn Sparks, became a familiar face.

One day Katelyn told her she was about 33 weeks pregnant. She hadn't had any prenatal care, and she couldn't take care of another baby.

D'Amber had looked into adoption before, just not this soon. She asked if she could take him anyway. "Not knowing nothing. I just know I got to get this baby."

She found Katelyn a doctor and took her to her first checkup. Nolan was born healthy, 7 pounds 8 ounces.

Katelyn is still in his life. "She's more or less like my big sister," she said.

"I thought he needed me, but I really needed him."
