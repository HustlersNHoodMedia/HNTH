Angel Murdock was on Netflix's Temptation Island. For almost two years after that, she built an audience of 34,000 posting as a student in Duke's physician assistant program. Study nights. Clinical rotations. What's in my PA school bag. Photos in scrubs outside the program sign on South Duke Street. People started coming to her for advice on how to get in.

On August 20 a Reddit user who said he had just graduated from that program asked out loud who she was. Nobody in the class had ever seen her. Not in class, not on campus, not at a single program event.

Then a woman named Kiera Brown answered. Different person. She is one of the 91 who actually started that class of 2026, and she was the class historian, which means keeping the record of who was there was literally her job. She put out eight minutes of receipts. Six million people watched it.

Inc. asked Duke directly. Duke Health wrote back that they can find no record of anyone by that name ever applying to or attending the program.

Murdock has not said one word. She deleted her Instagram and her TikTok and went quiet.

Duke's is the oldest physician assistant program in the country. It is the one that created the profession. Kiera Brown is the first in her family to finish college. She says she noticed during her first year and said nothing, posted 40 straight weeks of her rotations, took no summer break, got hooded at Duke Chapel on August 7, and only then opened her mouth.

Her words: too busy actually earning the degree to comment.
