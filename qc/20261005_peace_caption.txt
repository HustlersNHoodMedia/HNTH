Peace Prophet sells her paintings on the sidewalk in downtown Chicago. The small ones go for $5. She's 71, and she has been living on the street since 2000.

Before that she was Janice Carol Rivers. She graduated from DePaul in 1978 with a degree in sociology and did social work at the Ida B. Wells projects. Her daughter says she helped write the grant that got the kids there a new playground.

She paints every day, right there on the street, and buys her supplies at the art store down the block. "I'm doing this art thing, hustling around," she said.

A man named Francis Fullam started buying her work in 2012. He figures he owns close to 1,000 pieces now, and he mostly pays her in art-supply gift cards. Her work is in a museum collection, and a gallery in Michigan has sold it for as much as $600.

Earlier this year her children found her. On her 71st birthday her daughter called on FaceTime and her granddaughter sang to her. "It's the best birthday I ever had," she said.
