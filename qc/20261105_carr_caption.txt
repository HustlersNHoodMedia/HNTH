Chris Carr made it to the final round of the 1997 Slam Dunk Contest. An 18-year-old Kobe Bryant took the trophy home.

Almost 30 years later, his family still won't let him live it down. His daughter dug up the photo of Kobe holding the trophy, with Chris in the back of the shot, and it goes in the family group chat every year.

His response never changes: "Y'all are hilarious."

This June, the joke turned into something else. His son Cameron, 21, went 24th overall in the NBA Draft and ended up with the Los Angeles Lakers, Kobe's team. He wears No. 43 as a tribute to his dad.

"Deep down, I've always wanted to be better than him."
