Teyana Taylor is officially a culinary school graduate.

She enrolled at the Auguste Escoffier School of Culinary Arts in July 2025, right in the middle of the biggest year of her acting career. In January, with assignments due, her instructor gave her a few extra days so she could make it to the Golden Globes. She went, and she won Best Supporting Actress for One Battle After Another.

"I'm a straight-A student, and I want to keep it that way," she said.

This month she finished the program and earned her Diploma in Culinary Arts & Operations, with her daughter there to see it. Cooking has been her thing since she was, in her words, the "hood Betty Crocker" in Harlem.
