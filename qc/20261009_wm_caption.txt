DeMarcus Dickerson has been selling water and Gatorade on the same Memphis corner for years, rain or shine. Drivers call him the Watermane.

On Tuesday police told him to get out of the street and onto the sidewalk. Four hours later they came back, found him in the street again and arrested him. Before they took him in he asked them to leave his cooler and his wagon at the fire station.

The next day his brother Tony was standing in his spot, selling. "When we were young, we didn't have that much money," Tony said. "So that's why we had to find a way to make money to try to have a job, feed ourselves and stuff like that at a young age."

Cars rolled by honking "Free the Watermane!" Two people pulled up with cases of water. The DA dropped the charge and said prosecution wasn't warranted.

Thursday he was back on his corner, handing drinks up to a fire truck.
