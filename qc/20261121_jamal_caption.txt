Jamal Hinton was a high school senior in Arizona in 2016 when a text came in from a number he didn't know. It was Wanda Dench, inviting her grandson to Thanksgiving dinner. She had the wrong number.

He texted back, "You not my grandma," and then asked the real question: "Can I still get a plate tho?"

Her answer: "Of course you can. That's what grandma's do...feed every one."

He showed up that year, and he never stopped. He was there after her husband Lonnie passed in 2020, and through her breast cancer in 2024. Last year was Thanksgiving number 10.

This week Jamal announced the two of them signed with Lifetime for a movie about their friendship. He called Wanda "a bonus grandma in my life."
