It was her birthday and she was on the clock when her sons started walking through the door, one behind the other, and every single one of them had flowers in his hands.

By the time they were done she had more bouquets than arms. Roses, bright ones, a balloon sticking out the top. She didn't put a single one down.

Her coworkers stood there watching. Somebody kept filming. She just kept laughing with her eyes closed.

Her sons posted it that night with one line: happy birthday mommy we love you.
