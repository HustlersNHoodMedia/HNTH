Jadakiss says he's thinking about going back to school for business, so Kiss Café is set up right when he passes it down to his kids. He's not picky about where either: Ivy League, an HBCU, community college or online.

Kiss Café is a real family business. Jadakiss launched it in 2022 with his father, Bob Phillips, who got into coffee in 1977 at General Foods and later spent years importing green coffee. His son Jaewon, a college graduate, runs the marketing and has worked next to his grandfather since 2018.

When they launched, Bob put it simple: "I think it's every parent's dream to have their kids follow them."
