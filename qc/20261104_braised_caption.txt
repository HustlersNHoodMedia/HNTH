Kevin Lee once fought for a UFC title. When his fighting days were over, he went to the kitchen. He was raised by a dad who could really cook.

In 2024 he opened a little stand called Braised at a farmers market in Hollywood, Florida, and asked his younger brother Keith not to promote it.

Keith had somebody else go buy the food, tasted it in his car and posted it anyway. The next day the stand sold out in three hours. Keith also gave him $10,000 to put back into the business.

The stand turned into a food truck. On Sunday it turned into a restaurant in Dallas, and Keith was right there next to him for the grand opening, handing out free plates to the first 30 people in line.
