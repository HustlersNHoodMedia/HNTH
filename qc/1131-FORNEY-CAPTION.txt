First week of kindergarten. A 5 year old in Forney, Texas got on the Arka Montessori bus after school on August 14 to go to aftercare, and like any tired little kid on day four, she fell asleep in her seat.

Nobody woke her up. Nobody counted heads. The bus got parked and locked with her still inside, and it was over 100 degrees out.

Two hours later her big sister showed up to pick her up and the daycare told her the girl was not there. That is when her mother, Tidankay Tejan, came and started searching. The family's lawyer says the girl heard her mama's voice from inside that bus and started banging on the door. That is how they found her. She was taken to the hospital and treated for dehydration.

Her father, Sorie Tejan, said it plain: "I need justice." Texas law requires childcare centers to account for every single child they transport. Forney police confirmed an arrest in the case.

The family is not releasing their daughter's name. Three weeks after a 6 year old in Memphis died the exact same way, this one lived.
