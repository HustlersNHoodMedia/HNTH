A 92 year old walked out to the speakers at the cookout and started dancing by himself.

Plates were out, music was up, and everybody else stayed in their chairs. Grandkids, nieces, grown men half his age, all of them sat there and watched him work.

He found the pocket in the beat and stayed in it. Nobody got up with him. He did not look like he was waiting on anybody to.

Somebody in the family kept the camera on him, and that is the only reason the rest of us got to see it.
