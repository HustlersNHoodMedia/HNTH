The storm hit Gary on August 11 with winds near 100 mph. It knocked out power for more than 370,000 customers. Almost two weeks later 8,600 homes were still sitting in the dark.

Trae Tha Truth drove up from Houston with Relief Gang. They cut trees off houses and off power lines, brought in an 18 wheeler of water, diapers, formula and cleaning supplies, and ran hot food for the blocks the trucks never reached. Seniors who could not walk to a distribution center got theirs delivered.

Gary is Michael Jackson's hometown. It is also 76 percent Black, and Freddie Gibbs, who grew up there, put it plain: this was not a problem that started with the storm.

If you are in Gary and still need help with outages, flood damage or fallen trees, dial 211.
