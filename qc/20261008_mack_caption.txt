Brian Mack cut hair in Charlotte for 26 years before highway construction pushed his barbershop out. His son B.J. grew up in that shop.

In 2024, B.J. was playing college ball at South Carolina. He took his NIL money and put it into reopening his dad's shop. Brian didn't want to take it. "No father really wants to take his son's money," he said. House of Hoops opened that summer on Little Rock Road.

This June, Brian ran a Father's Day weekend shoe drive for kids who don't have a father in the stands. They gave away more than 50 pairs. The leftovers were still in the shop on September 12 when a group of masked teens smashed the front door at 2:52 in the morning and took the shoes and the clippers.

Brian ran with a gang when he was young. He didn't ask for revenge. He asked them to come in, apologize, and work Saturdays sweeping the shop.

"The last thing I want to do is see another kid lost in the system."

A barber supply company in Atlanta has already sent him new clippers.
