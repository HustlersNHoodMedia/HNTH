Jalen Rose never met his father in person.

His dad was Jimmy Walker, the No. 1 pick in the 1967 NBA Draft. His mom, Jeanne Rose, raised him on her own in Detroit.

When he was born, she gave him a name nobody around them had heard before. She took "Ja" from James, his father's name, and "len" from Leonard, her brother, the one who drove her to the hospital to have him.

When Jalen got drafted in 1994, he was the first Jalen in the NBA. When he retired in 2007, he was still the only one.

Now the league is full of them, and so is the NFL.

"For anybody that's ever heard the name Jalen, she created it," he said.

Jeanne passed in February 2021 after fighting cancer. That Mother's Day he talked about what she used to tell him.

"She was like, 'You're going to make it, you're going to get us out the hood, I promise you.' And I was like, 'I'm going to do it, ma.' And I did it. We did it."
