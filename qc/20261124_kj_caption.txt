Kajavias "KJ" Sanford has been working since middle school, when his first job was sweeping up at a barbershop. He grew up to drive an 18-wheeler in Memphis and became the main provider for his five kids, ages 4 to 17.

About three months ago he woke up and everything was blurry. He thought he was still tired, so he lay back down. His sight never came back.

Doctors diagnosed him with Leber's Hereditary Optic Neuropathy, a rare inherited condition that takes away central vision. He had to give up driving.

"I have my peripheral, but central vision, to where reading, identifying faces, driving, things like that, it hinders everything," he said.

He applied for disability, but that can take a while. A neighbor helped him set up a fundraiser, and what he wants right now is to take care of his kids and give them a good Christmas.
