Austin Collins was dropping his kids off at the school bus stop in St. Peters, Missouri, when an SUV rolled out of a parking lot, down a hill and into a pond. A 3-year-old girl named Melody was still inside.

Her mom had stepped out to help her other kids onto the bus. They were the ones who screamed when they saw the car rolling backward.

Austin called 911 and jumped in. He didn't know how deep the water was, so he held onto the SUV to stay up. Someone handed him a hammer. He broke the window, cut his hand clearing out the glass, and pulled Melody out.

"I just saw a big ol' splash, and immediately, everyone just ran over and took action," he said.

Melody is doing fine. Her mom says that without him, they would have lost her. A GoFundMe was started to help the family replace the car.
