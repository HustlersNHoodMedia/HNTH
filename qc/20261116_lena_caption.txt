With Thanksgiving coming up, Lena Waithe just started the family argument early.

On The Vic Mensa Show, the creator of The Chi said being someone's parent doesn't automatically earn a seat in your life.

"I don't believe you should go to your parents' house out of obligation. You should go out of a desire, out of a want," she said. "Blood is not entitlement."

Some people agreed right away. They say nobody should be guilted into sitting at a holiday table where they don't feel welcome.

Others pushed back hard. They say family is family, your parents raised you, and you don't get to cut them off just because it's uncomfortable.

So where do you stand? Do you go home for the holidays because you want to, or because you have to?
