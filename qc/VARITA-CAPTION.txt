A woman pulled up to the Wendy's window and handed the girl working the drive thru a little magic wand.

Not a tip, not a complaint about the order. A plastic star on a stick with a wrist strap on it.

The girl took it, held it up, turned it over in her hands and stood there grinning at it with the headset still on her ear.

The woman who gave it to her wrote underneath: "you might want to make more."
