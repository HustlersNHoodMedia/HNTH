When Jalen Brunson signed with the Knicks in 2022, his dad Rick joined the coaching staff that same summer. People said that's how he got the deal and called him a nepo baby. His mom Sandra saved every one of those comments.

She's sent Jalen a message before every game for 15 years. It started in high school, when his dad left for a coaching job in North Carolina. The night before each game she printed out a few words and taped them to his bathroom mirror, the fridge and his lunch bag. In college the notes became texts, and they never stopped.

In June, Jalen won Finals MVP and brought the Knicks their first title since 1973.

Before his first game as a champion, she skipped the text and wrote him a letter instead. "You're still my baby boy, so you still have to listen to me."
