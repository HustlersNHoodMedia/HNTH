Frederick Engram Jr. was 12, he'd just been invited to a camp for the best young football prospects, and he was saving up for his first camera.

At the beginning of April he was playing football with his friends at school when something didn't feel right. It was a spinal cord stroke. "My whole right leg was paralyzed. I cannot walk," he said. Doctors told the family he'd be in a walker or a wheelchair for six months to a year. He was walking in about a month.

While he couldn't play, he got the camera. He started shooting the other kids from the sideline and put out a price list. 10 photos for $20. "Every time I step onto a field with my camera, I'm proving to myself that anything is possible," he wrote.

Now he's playing flag football while he gets his strength back, and his mama Trisha believes he'll be back in pads next year. "God really put me in the situation for a reason," Frederick said. "He put me in the situation to never give up."
