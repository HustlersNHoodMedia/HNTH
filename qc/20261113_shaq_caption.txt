This kid grew up on Shaq's highlights. His dad always told him he'd meet him one day.

On July 3rd, his dad's shop, Effortless Motors, delivered Shaq's one-of-one '64 Impala, and the kid was standing right next to him. Shaq looked at him and told him he was one of the youngest members of the Diesel Dog Mafia now, and that meant he needed a chain. He even asked him what he wanted on it.

July turned into September. Nothing came, and the kid started thinking Shaq forgot about him.

Then one morning there was a box at the door. From Shaq. Inside was his chain, fully iced out.

His dad put it best: "As a father, moments like this are priceless."
