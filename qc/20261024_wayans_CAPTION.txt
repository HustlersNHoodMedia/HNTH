Keenen Ivory Wayans was the second of 10 kids, raised by Howell and Elvira Wayans in a small apartment in the Fulton projects in Manhattan. His pops managed a supermarket and his mama was a homemaker and a social worker.

When Keenen created In Living Color in 1990, he didn't come up by himself. Damon and Kim were in the cast. Shawn was the DJ, SW-1. Marlon joined later.

"My brothers have been telling me what to do my whole life, but at least I'm getting paid for it now!" Marlon said.

In 2025 the NAACP Image Awards put the whole Wayans family in its Hall of Fame.
