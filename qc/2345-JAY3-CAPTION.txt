Somebody in dentistry left this under one of Jaylen A. Johnson's videos: "You are a dentist? Dude you are ghetto."

Jaylen is a dentist. Morehouse first, then his doctorate at Tufts. Red loupes, hands in somebody's mouth all day.

He answered with the receipts. Him in the clinic in scrubs. Him in the white coat ceremony with his class. Him at the podium at his own graduation. His people in the crowd, his mother in his arms.

"This 'ghetto' first generation dentist worked hard for EVERYTHING he currently has and will never change or conform for ANYONE."

First generation. Nobody in his house had done it before him.

That video passed 1.8 million views. The man who left the comment then posted publicly that calling someone's hair ghetto is offensive and carries racist connotations, and that he takes accountability for it. In private he told Jaylen he had misunderstood him.

His line about all of it: no matter how many diplomas you get, how many practices you build, how hard you work to live the life you deserve, some people will still look at you and see a you know what.

3.8 percent of dentists in this country are Black.
