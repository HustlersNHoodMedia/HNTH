TJ Malveaux is from Morgan City, Louisiana. In middle school he taught himself to cut hair because he needed a few dollars. By 2006 he had his license.

He didn't start with a shop. He started with a bag. He'd go to LSU and cut athletes and coaches wherever they were: the facilities, the dorm rooms. Word got around the locker room, the waitlist got long, and the demand turned into Draft Picks Barbershop.

The name came from a client. Davon Godchaux used to sit in his chair asking to get "draft day fresh."

Jamal Adams. Ben Simmons. Trendon Watford. Players from LSU, Southern and the Saints. Now Draft Picks is the official barbershop of LSU Football, with its own room inside the football building, and every Thursday his team goes in and gets the Tigers right for Saturday.

Three shops in Louisiana. The fourth opens October 1 in Houma.

"They made my dream come true."
