Kiara Bowling was walking her best friend's dog in Oakland in August when a car came at her. Police said two brothers, 6 and 4, had taken their parents' keys and were driving it.

When paramedics got to her, she had no pulse. Brain injury, broken ribs, a fractured face, weeks unconscious. A few weeks before, she had been crowned Miss California Regency and was finishing college with plans for law school. Now she had to learn how to eat, talk and walk all over again.

Three months later she went back to that same sidewalk on her own two feet, with a cane. She doesn't remember the crash. She says she isn't angry at those boys, and that she forgives them.

"I feel blessed to be here and to still be able to walk."
