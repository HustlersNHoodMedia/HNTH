Kapri DeRamus and Tyasia Thomas were supposed to get married on September 5 at a venue in Altamonte Springs, Florida. Six years together. Everybody already there.

He was driving over when a car crossed into his lane and hit him head-on. Both arms broken. Jaw broken. A rib broken. A deep cut to his knee.

"I'm just in my own little world, and out of nowhere, man, just got struck by a car. I was so delirious."

Tyasia got the call about an hour before the ceremony. She drove straight to the hospital with her whole family behind her.

"Just seeing him in that state was very, very traumatizing. I remember in the car just saying, my biggest fear is losing him."

Once he was stabilized, they did not reschedule anything. They pulled everybody into a multipurpose room at the hospital that same night. His grandfather officiated. He said his vows from the bed, both arms wrapped.

"Throughout all of the trauma that I'm dealing with, I want to have this ring on. It signifies our love. Six strong years, and a lifetime ahead of us."

He got released Wednesday.
