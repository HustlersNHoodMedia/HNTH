Big Pun would have turned 55 on Tuesday. His son Chris Rivers was 6 when he passed in 2000.

For his dad's birthday, Chris didn't post an old photo. He went looking for the one person who knew exactly how to make Pun's favorite meal.

Plot twist: it was his mom, Liza Rios.

No restaurant, no celebrity chef. Fried chicken cutlets, mashed potatoes and sweet corn, the same plate she used to make for her husband at home.

Chris tasted it on camera and gave it a 9 out of 10. Somebody tell him that's his mama's cooking he's grading.
