Disney offered Jason Weaver $2 million upfront to sing young Simba. He was 13.

His mama, Kitty Haywood, managed him and raised him by herself in Chicago. She used to sing backup for Aretha Franklin, so she knew how this business moves. Every contract he got, she sat him down at the kitchen table after school and broke it down line by line. Then she asked him one question. What's your worth?

When the Lion King offer came, she told Disney no. She asked for royalties and a much smaller check upfront. Her thinking was simple. If they're willing to offer that much, imagine what it's going to make.

When the first royalty check came, they both thought it was a mistake. It wasn't. Three decades later it still comes.

Jason told the story on Funky Friday this week, and he said he can't thank her enough.

"If you just take anything that you're given, that's all you're ever gonna get."
