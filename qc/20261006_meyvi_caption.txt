Meyvilore's first paycheck as a nurse hit her bank account this week, and she filmed the moment, crying. "God is good."

It took years to see that deposit. She worked as a patient care aide, then a home health aide, then drawing blood, the jobs at the bottom of the hospital, while raising her family as a single mother. She says nobody knew what it took to get here.

Her page is full of reminders to the people still climbing. Keep going. If you fail, try again.

The comment section turned into a testimony service. The top comment came from a woman who was in 9th grade when her own mama got her first nurse check and called the kids into the room just to look at it. That mama has two master's degrees today.
