Keenen Ivory Wayans was the second of 10 kids, raised by Howell and Elvira Wayans in a small apartment in the Fulton projects in Manhattan.

He was on his way to an engineering degree at Tuskegee when he decided to do stand-up instead. His mama wasn't sold. "A comedian? I know you your whole life; you ain't never said nothing funny," she told him, the way Damon tells it.

Keenen went on to create In Living Color in 1990 and brought the family with him. Damon and Kim were in the cast, Shawn was the DJ, SW-1, and Marlon joined later.

In 2025 the NAACP Image Awards put the whole Wayans family in its Hall of Fame.
