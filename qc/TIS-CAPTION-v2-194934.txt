Tisha Campbell just told the story of how close she came to never being Gina.

In 1992 she was 23 and still living at her mom's house. She had just shot a pilot for another show, and it got picked up for 13 episodes. That's a real check, the kind of money that gets you your own place. Then Martin Lawrence asked her to be on his show, and all he had was a pilot. She told him no.

Martin wasn't taking no. "Nah, nah, I'm coming over your house. Where do you live?" She told him she lived with her mom. He came anyway and told her to her face that he really wanted her for the role.

She knew it was a lot of money to walk away from. She walked away from it and went with Martin.

That show ran five seasons, and Martin and Gina became the couple a whole generation grew up on.
