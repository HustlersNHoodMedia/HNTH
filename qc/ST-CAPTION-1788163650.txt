Stormi Steele is from a town in Mississippi she describes as having no stoplight. She was 23 credit hours from an art degree with a 3.7 GPA when she walked away from it to do hair.

She moved to Huntsville, Alabama with $800 and started mixing a hair serum in her kitchen. Cayenne pepper in it, for the tingle on the scalp. She gave it to her clients at the salon. They kept asking for more, so she started selling it.

Canvas Beauty launched in 2018 out of a 500 square foot warehouse. Year one it moved over a million units. Year two she opened a storefront in a strip mall.

Then in 2022 the whole thing nearly went under. She has said a chief financial officer she trusted forged her signature on predatory loans while she was on bed rest with a high risk pregnancy.

She rebuilt it by selling live on TikTok herself, four hours a day, her own face, her own account.

On June 8, 2024, she sold a million dollars of product in a single livestream. First person on the platform to ever do it. She did it again in September in half the time, and on Black Friday she hit the million in the first two hours and finished over three.

The warehouse is 30,000 square feet now.
