She was an analyst on Wall Street who started making lipstick in her Brooklyn kitchen, because nothing on the shelf was made for her.

Shark Tank told her no in 2015. She built it anyway. The Lip Bar ended up in more than 2,000 stores, and she put up billboards in four cities clowning the show for passing on her.

Her second line, Thread Beauty, went into Target in 2022. Everything $8. It never caught the same way, and she thought about shutting it last year.

Instead she got on camera and said it plain: it's not working, which is kinda hard to say. Then she offered somebody else a piece of the company to come be its face. No money up front. Just equity.

All of this on top of the boycott over Target rolling back its DEI programs, which took 30 to 40% of her sales in there. She said the number out loud and still told folks not to go back.
