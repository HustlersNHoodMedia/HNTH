Trenton Lewis was 21, a single dad in Arkansas with a new job loading trucks at UPS and no car. His shift started at 4 a.m., so he left home around midnight and walked. About 11 miles a day, there and back, for seven months. This was 2018. He kept it to himself at work.

"I made it to work. I was never late," he said. "Doing this for my daughter, that's all."

A coworker everybody calls Mama Pat found out. She and her husband passed the hat around the building and bought him a car.

His first drive was to pick up his baby girl.
