In April 2023, Jamie Foxx was rushed to a hospital in Atlanta while filming a movie, and for months nobody knew what happened.

He finally explained it in his Netflix special: a brain bleed that led to a stroke. "I don't remember 20 days," he said.

This summer he admitted he was "a little on the fence" about having another baby at his age.

This week, at 58, he became a dad for the third time. His daughters Corinne and Anelise are 32 and 17.
