Sierra Nicole Reece had just finished an MBA abroad and was job hunting. She was making videos to build a portfolio in digital and marketing strategy, not to go viral.

On April 16 she was already in bed at her dad's place in New Jersey, bonnet on, glasses on. She recorded six seconds. "Forget Coachella. I'm trying to go to the Minnesota State Fair. They got brand deals for that?" Then she went to sleep.

It hit 4 million views overnight. The top comment was the Minnesota State Fair's own account: "I'm listening."

A Twin Cities talent manager reached out and helped her turn it into a real pitch. The fair covered admission. Visit Saint Paul covered the hotel, the food and a stipend. Explore Minnesota paid for her work time and expenses. The state agency gets hundreds of pitches a year and takes almost none of them.

She went at the end of August with two other Black creators, Sade in the Cities and FeedMeez.

"This is going to be three Black women covering a state fair from our perspective, which is like unheard of."
