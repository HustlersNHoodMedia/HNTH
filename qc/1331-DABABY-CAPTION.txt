DaBaby opened No Knife Chicken & Waffles in Charlotte, his own city. One plate on the menu runs $45.

What comes with it: a cornbread waffle, four wings, collard greens, candied yams and mac and cheese.

The internet went at him about the price. His answer: "This isn't just flour and water." And for anybody who wants cheap, he said go to Waffle House.

Charlotte is where he is from. He put a soul food plate on a rap-owned menu and priced it like a restaurant, not a corner spot.

So settle it. $45 for that plate, with all five of those sides on it. Would you pay it?
