Brock Morgan sent 223 job applications in two months. Number 223 hired him.

He's from Kannapolis, North Carolina. He went to Howard on the football team, played linebacker, and wrote the sports page for The Hilltop between practices. Two MEAC championship rings. All-Academic in 2023. Graduated magna cum laude. Then a master's from Medill at Northwestern.

By the time he started applying, he had already reported from Super Bowl LX, the opening of the Obama Presidential Center, and WNBA All-Star weekend. The rejection emails kept coming back with the same line: thank you for applying, but unfortunately. Not enough experience.

He never just hit submit. Every time he applied somewhere, he found somebody at that station and introduced himself, so the people deciding would know his name instead of a file.

That's the same thing he did at Howard, walking into businesses with a proposal in his hand until he had eleven NIL deals in one year.

FOX54 in Huntsville hired him as an anchor and reporter covering sports and news.

"Glory to God. After 223 applications."
