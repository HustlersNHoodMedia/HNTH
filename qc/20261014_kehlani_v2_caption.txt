Kehlani's mom gave birth to her little brother while she was locked up in California, handcuffed to a gurney. He passed five days later.

Kehlani kept that story to herself her whole life. She finally shared it inside that same prison, the women's prison in Chino, where the first episode of her new show Live From The Hole was filmed. She found out it happened there while they were filming.

It wasn't the first time prison took her mom away. When Kehlani was born, her mom got arrested at the hospital. Kehlani got raised by her aunt and grew up on visits, hearing her mama was "on vacation" or "at work."

She sat in a circle with the mothers locked up in there and walked out with a different picture of the woman she grew up angry at. "My mom probably wanted to raise me. She was just dealt a really crazy hand."
