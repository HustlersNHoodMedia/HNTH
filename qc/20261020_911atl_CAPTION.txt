Mildreth Medina was at work in Atlanta, with her sister, when the contractions started. She was only 34 weeks along.

"By 12:30, the pain did not go away. It was back to back already."

She ended up in a bathroom stall at a food hall, and her sister called 911. On the other end was dispatcher Alexandria Jean Pierre. Until a few weeks before, Atlanta's 911 center had to transfer medical calls like that one to another agency. The team had just been certified to handle them.

Alexandria stayed on the line and walked her through it, with senior dispatcher Mondrealle George right beside her. About 15 minutes after the call, the baby was born.

"With one push, literally she was out."

The baby is still in the hospital, and her mama says she's doing well.
