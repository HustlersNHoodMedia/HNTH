This is Deon Cole's mother, Charleen. She passed in September 2021.

Deon is her only child. He called her "my only rock."

A year later, the only day he could film his Netflix special was the anniversary of her death. He filmed it anyway, and he changed the name to "Charleen's Boy" so her name would ring forever.

This weekend was her birthday. Deon was in Rome with two friends, so he took her to dinner too. Her picture sat on a phone in the middle of the table, between the wine glasses.

"Happy birthday mommy!!! I love u and miss u."
