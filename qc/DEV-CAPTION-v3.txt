In 2013 Devin Allen was standing in line at a Best Buy in Baltimore, telling his grandma he wanted to be a photographer. Everybody else had laughed at him. His friends told him he was too old, that the window for art school had closed. "They told me I didn't know anything about art," he said.

His grandma Doris asked him one thing: "Are you going to stick with this?" He promised her. She put the camera on her credit card.

Two years later, his photo from the Baltimore uprising ran on the cover of TIME. He was only the third amateur photographer ever to land one.

A few years ago he took over the West Baltimore house she bought with her savings in 1978, the house he was born in. Going through her things, he found photo albums. Hundreds of pictures of the family, from the '70s all the way to the 2000s.

"That's when I realized my grandma was the first photographer I ever knew."

Those pictures are his new solo show, "She Saw Me Coming," up in Baltimore through December 4.
