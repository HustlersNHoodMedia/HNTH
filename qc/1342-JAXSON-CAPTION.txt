Jaxson Maison is 6 and has had long hair his whole life. His cousin Mason Jones is 13 and is fighting acute myeloid leukemia for the second time. Mason was first diagnosed at 11. This round he is getting treatment in Texas, far from Loganville, Georgia, where the two of them grew up around each other.

Jaxson asked his dad, Rodney, to shave his head. Rodney asked him if he was sure. He was.

"I just felt like I wanted to do it for my cousin. He has cancer. I wanted to show him that I'm not the only one who would do it."

Rodney filmed it. Then Jaxson FaceTimed Mason to show him.

"It made me cry, but it was happy tears," Mason said. "He didn't have to do that, but he did, and it really made me happy."

Mason's parents, Chris and Christine, are staying by his side in Texas. The family has a GoFundMe up for the treatment. Jaxson's hair will grow back. What he did for his cousin will not need to.
