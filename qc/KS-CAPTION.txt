Kahh Spence spent over a decade doing hair for Kelly Rowland, Cardi B, Kehlani, Normani and Victoria Monét. Everybody figured his own business would be haircare. He said he didn't have anything valuable to say in haircare yet.

He made a perfume instead. He named it Samir Grey after his middle name and his favorite color, and he built it for his mama.

Her name was Sharisee. She died of colon cancer in 2017. She was in a wheelchair at the end. They were all supposed to go to Hawaii together, Kehlani was going to fly her out for a show, and it never happened.

What he told the perfumer was that he wanted it to smell like his last dream of her. The two of them walking on a beach. "My mom was in a wheelchair when she passed. So us walking really meant a lot."

He didn't call in any favors when he launched it. He wanted the thing to stand on its own.

In April, Tina Knowles posted a video of Kelly Rowland getting her hair done with the bottle sitting in frame. Two weeks of inventory was gone in two hours. It stayed sold out for five weeks.

Over $500,000 so far. His first fragrance brand, back in 2019, he started with the lottery money his mother left him.
