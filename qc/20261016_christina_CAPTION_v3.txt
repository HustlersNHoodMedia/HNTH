Christina is a single mom with three little ones, a 4 year old, a 2 year old and a 5 month old baby girl. No car, no place of their own. She's been going from shelter to shelter in Los Angeles trying to find somewhere safe for them to sleep.

She was in the store with all three kids in the cart when a stranger walked up holding two jars of baby food and asked if she had a couple of quarters. She didn't have quarters. She handed him a $5 bill.

The stranger was Jimmy Darts, and right there he handed her $500.

When he asked why she helped him with everything she's going through, she said: "You never know what somebody else is going through. If I can keep a smile on somebody else's face, I don't mind at all."

Her dream is to work in the medical field as a surgical tech.

Then Jimmy posted her story and opened a fundraiser. In one day, more than 2,000 people sent over $42,000 to get her family into a home and a car.
