An 8-year-old in Memphis told his family he didn't want any gifts for his birthday. He wanted to feed people instead.

So Kentintton McNeil spent the day downtown handing out chicken tacos and bottles of water to people living on the street. His family even pushed back his birthday trip so he could do it.

This is the second year in a row. The first time, 7 people came through. This year it was nearly three dozen.

His mama, his auntie, his granny, his cousin and his sister were right there serving with him, and so was his principal.

He said he wanted everybody to feel joyful about their food.

"I want them to feel happy."
