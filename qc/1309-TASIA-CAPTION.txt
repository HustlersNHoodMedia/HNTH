Tasia Fortune was 29 and had four kids. In August she was found hanging from a tree behind a vacant house on Road of Remembrance in Jackson, Mississippi.

For a month her family had no answers. In Mississippi, with the history that state carries, people all over the country were watching how this one got handled.

Thursday evening the state medical examiner ruled her death a h*micide.

Friday, Jackson police chief RaShall Brackney-Wheelock said officers had search and arrest warrants ready and picked up 51-year-old Jacques Ratliff while he was walking down the street. She said he knew Tasia. She would not talk about motive.

"We understand the questions and the desire for answers given Mississippi's history. We understand this case is being viewed through a broader lens, a global lens, and we do not dismiss those concerns. We are taking them seriously."

The investigation is still open and the chief said more arrests are likely.

Her mother, Christy Spivey, on the moment police told her: "I just started crying and told them thank you, thank you, thank you."

"We still don't have a lot of answers."
