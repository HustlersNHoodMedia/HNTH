Mojo Brookzz's first Netflix special, "I Know You Lying," is the number one show on Netflix in America this week.

His first comedy show had four people in the audience. His mama, his cousins and two people he didn't know.

Before comedy he was a party promoter who did private investigator work on the side. Two days before his sister died of cancer in 2013, she told him to give comedy a try. A few months later he was making sketches, and not long after that he was on stage.

One night he bombed in front of about 300 people and a heckler told him to get off the stage. He kept going.

His last tour sold more than 100,000 tickets. Last month he sold out Chicago's Wintrust Arena and had to add a second night, and his city named September 12 Mojo Brookzz Day.

"I'm proud of what I created," he wrote this week.
