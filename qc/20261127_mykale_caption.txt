Mykale Baker had just walked the stage in Georgia in May 2025. He stopped by the Burger King where he works, saw his coworkers getting slammed and jumped on the line. No uniform, not on the clock, medals still around his neck.

He took that job to save for college.

A mom named Maria Mendoza was in the drive-thru after her own daughter's graduation. She filmed him, posted it and started a fundraiser. It passed $230K, and Burger King added a $10K scholarship.

When she showed him the page he said, "Wow. I might go to college now."

He enrolled in a technical college to become a mechanic.
