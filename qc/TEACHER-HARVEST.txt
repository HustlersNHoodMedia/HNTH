Somebody posted a video about a letter her teacher wrote her and asked people what their own favorite teacher did. Almost seven thousand people answered. Reading them back is a strange thing.

"She bought me a yearbook because I told her about my family's financial struggles."

"She paid for the school trip so I could go with them."

"She took care of me when I was sick, and when my parents wouldn't pick me up she let me stay in her class all day."

"She took me and my friends outside for a talk and listened for three hours. That was the moment I realized she did something my own parents didn't. Listening without judging."

"In third grade my father was in and out of prison. She's the only one who noticed. She had breakfast with me before school so we could catch up."

One of them just said: noticed. Thirteen thousand people liked that one word.

A teacher gets paid to teach. Nobody pays them to be the only adult who saw you.
