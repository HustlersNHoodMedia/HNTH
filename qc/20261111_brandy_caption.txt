Sy'Rai Smith sang the national anthem at the Lakers game Friday night, her first time doing it at Crypto.com Arena. Her mom Brandy was courtside filming it on her phone.

Brandy opened this year singing the anthem at the NBA All-Star Game. Nine months later she watched her daughter take the same song to center court in Los Angeles.

After the game Brandy posted: "I saw the little girl I held in my arms and the woman standing before me… and felt every year of loving you all at once."

Uncle Ray J kept it short in the comments: "My little niece!!!"
