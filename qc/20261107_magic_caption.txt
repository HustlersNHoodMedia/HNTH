Magic Johnson married Cookie on September 14, 1991. Six weeks later, the Lakers team doctor called him in and told him he had tested positive for HIV.

Cookie was pregnant with their son, EJ. Magic has said telling her was the hardest thing he ever had to do. He told her she could leave him, and that he would understand.

She didn't go anywhere. She told him they were going to get down on their knees and pray, and they were going to figure it out together.

On November 7, 1991, he told the world. Back then most people heard HIV and thought death sentence.

Cookie and EJ were both fine. Magic is 67 now. In September, he and Cookie celebrated 35 years of marriage, and he marked 35 years since that press conference.

Years later, Cookie told Oprah what she did the night he told her: "I slapped him and said, 'Are you kidding? No. I love you.'"
