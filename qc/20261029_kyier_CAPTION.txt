Ky'ier Montgomery is 3 years old, and his grandma is the one he calls "Poopie."

When she collapsed at home in Southaven, Mississippi, he grabbed his iPad and called his mama.

"I called my mommy and said, 'My Poopie passed out, my Poopie passed out,'" he said.

His grandfather called 911 on his way back to the house, and when the dispatcher got Ky'ier on the line, he stayed on the whole time, telling them how she was doing and watching the front door for help.

His mama Jhakiya said she still doesn't know where he learned to do that. When the first responders walked up, he was yelling, "Come on, doctors, come on, doctors, help my granny, help my granny."

His grandma has been released from the hospital and she's recovering well. Ky'ier hasn't left her side, iPad in hand.
