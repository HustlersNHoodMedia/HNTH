Stephanie Mills told Shannon Sharpe the best thing that ever happened to her was her son, Farad.

She knew while she was pregnant that he had Down syndrome, and it didn't change a thing for her. "I told my doctor I didn't care if my son had one eye. That we were going to rock this world together."

She hit the ground running. When she mainstreamed him in school, she sent a teacher with him every day so he could keep up. She said she knew people would look at him like he couldn't do things, and she wasn't going to let that happen.

Farad was an A student. He wrote a children's book, The Adventures of Farad J., he already has a second one written, and he plans to go back to college for a degree in writing and reading.

She doesn't leave him at home either. He travels with her everywhere and he's part of her show. He's the one who introduces her on stage.

She even said she wouldn't be married today if her son didn't like her husband.
