Floyd Mayweather is coming out of retirement for a real fight. Ryan Garcia says it's not an exhibition, it's a title fight, with Floyd's 50-0 record on the line.

It's been a rough year on paper. In April the IRS filed a $7.3 million tax lien against him in Las Vegas. In June he was charged with two felonies after prosecutors said a $200,000 check he wrote for a luxury watch bounced. And a promoter is suing to get back $4.65 million in advances.

Floyd has always laughed off the broke talk. Asked about it last year, he said: "I'm too busy counting money."
