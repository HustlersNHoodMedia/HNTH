Terry Key grew up in Dellway, in East Nashville. When he told people he was going to start a bike club out there, the answer came back quick.

"They said, ooh, Dellway. Ain't no way."

He did it anyway. Hands On Nashville gave him his first 40 bikes. That was the start of Music City Riders.

Today the count is past 7,000 free bikes, handed out in Dellway and Edgehill to kids in the neighborhood. Some of the ones he rode with early on have since finished high school and gone to college.

This week it was Joshua's turn. He's 16. "My first time having a bike," he said.

A neighbor watching it happen called it priceless.

"I grew up out there," Terry said. "I'm not going to give up on somewhere that I grew up."
