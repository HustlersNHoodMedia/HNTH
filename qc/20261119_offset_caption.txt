Offset brought his 11-year-old daughter Kalea on stage for "Hating On Me" on The Tonight Show Starring Jimmy Fallon on Sunday.

The song is about the people who turned on him. In the middle of the performance, Offset sat on the couch with his head down. Kalea reached over, lifted his head back up and then leaned on his shoulder while the dancers kept moving around them.

The clip has been all over the timeline since.

"Hating On Me" is off GMFU, the new album he dropped Nov. 13, with features from Quavo, Gunna and Don Toliver.
