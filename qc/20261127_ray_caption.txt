His stepdad's name was Ray. Quiet man. Worked nights at a machine shop and never said much about how he felt.

When Ray passed, the stepson and his mama went to the shop to pick up his things. Taped inside the lid of the old lunchbox were three photos of the stepson. One was from his college graduation. He had no idea Ray kept it.

Then a coworker told him that on every break, Ray opened that lunchbox and bragged about his son.

He said he had always introduced Ray as "my mom's husband." At the shop, they only knew him as Ray's son.

The story spread from Reddit this week.
