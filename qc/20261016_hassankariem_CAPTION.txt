Hassan Kariem was 42. For more than 12 years he was Mr. Kariem at Wesley International Academy in Atlanta, teaching tech and coaching the robotics team.

He never had kids of his own. He met his fiancée's daughter when she was 3 and raised her as his. His family says it plain: his stepdaughter is his daughter.

He beat cancer twice. A few weeks ago, he and his daughter made it out of a car fire.

On Monday he went to Six Flags Over Georgia for his niece's birthday and sat next to her on the Goliath. Witnesses say his lap bar came loose near the top, and he was thrown from the ride. His niece was not hurt. Investigators still haven't said what went wrong, and the ride is closed.

When the news broke, a mom recognized him. Her little girl used to cry every morning at school drop off, and he was the man waiting at the door to walk her in. Months later he told her, "She's done really good."

She never knew his name until this week.

His family is asking people to watch out for fake fundraisers using his name.
