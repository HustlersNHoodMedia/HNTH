When Vince Carter left North Carolina for the NBA in 1998, he made his mom, Michelle, a promise: he was going to finish his degree.

He kept taking classes between seasons, and by May 2001 he had earned it.

The graduation ceremony landed on the same day as Game 7 of the Eastern Conference semifinals against Allen Iverson and the 76ers.

He walked in Chapel Hill that morning, went straight to the airport, took a chartered plane to Philadelphia and got to the arena hours before tip-off.

That night he had 20 points, 9 assists and 7 rebounds. His last shot at the buzzer missed, and the Raptors lost 88-87.

People blamed the graduation for years. He never backed down.

"I would do it again if I had to."
