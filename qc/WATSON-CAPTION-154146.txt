Judge Warren Watson was born in Pittsburgh in 1923. He came out of Westinghouse High School in 1940, the same Homewood school that gave jazz Billy Strayhorn, Erroll Garner and Ahmad Jamal. Then the Navy, 1943 to 1946. Then Duquesne, the bar, and a seat on the Allegheny County bench starting in 1966.

He is 103 now. Retired from the courtroom, not from the stage. His band is called Court in Session, and on September 5 he played the Monroeville Jazz Festival with them, Omega Psi Phi cap on, electric wind instrument in hand.

The people who put the festival on believe nobody older is still playing jazz live anywhere in the country. Nobody has proven it yet. He does not seem to be waiting on it.

He said it best a few years back, when somebody asked why he still plays: when he feels down, he picks it up and blows it out of his system.
