Nafees Norris grew up on Estaugh Street off 20th and Tioga. He wanted to be a scientist since he was seven and gave up on it, because nobody on TV or in the neighborhood looked like a scientist. He says he only went to college to stay out of trouble.

When they asked him to pick a major he asked what the hardest thing they had was. They said nursing or biology. He took biology.

His family got put out of their house on that same block. In 2020 he became the first African American accepted into Thomas Jefferson University's Biopharmaceutical Process Engineering master's program, and he did not celebrate it. His words: I got in, but I got to graduate. I got to set the bar.

He walked in a suit his father sewed in the 1970s and re-tailored to his size, because Nafees never made it to his senior prom.

Then he left the lab job in Delaware and started Nafees Innovations, putting North Philly kids in real lab coats with real equipment.
