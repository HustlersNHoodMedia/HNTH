Dorian Jackson was the oldest of eight. Greensboro, Dudley High, third generation Aggie at North Carolina A&T. Honors College, student government, Alpha Phi Alpha. He had a job waiting at Wells Fargo for after graduation in May.

On Saturday evening he was kayaking on Lake Norman with his younger sister. Her kayak flipped about 30 yards from a dock and she was struggling. He paddled over and tried to pull her onto his kayak. She had a life jacket on. He didn't.

Neighbors jumped in and got her to shore. He went under and did not come back up. Crews found him the next afternoon.

He was 21.

The university put it plainly in the letter to families: he passed away saving his sister from drowning.
