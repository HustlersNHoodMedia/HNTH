Roy Lee Campbell Jr. hasn't driven his car since August 6.

He was headed home from a night shift in Fairfield County, Ohio when deputies pulled him over for a license plate light and a turn signal. He handed over a state ID. They ran it. Nothing came back on him. Then they asked for his license.

Campbell, 48, drives a 2000 Blazer he calls his work car. He says a K-9 was walked around it and the search went on for about an hour. His driver door had been broken for a long time, so he gets in and out through the passenger side. He told them that. A deputy pulled it open anyway, and it came apart. Another one jammed the door he actually used.

The sheriff's own incident report says the search was conducted and nothing was located. He was handed a warning for the turn signal and the plate light.

He's looking for a lawyer. The car hasn't moved since.
