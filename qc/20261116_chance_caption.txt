Chance the Rapper is a girl dad three times over now. He announced the birth of his daughter Hayli on Sunday, celebrating with her mom.

"Welcome to the world Hayli. Mom n Dad love you. God is with you."

The carousel had it all: Hayli in the hospital, a maternity shoot in front of a waterfall, and the belly painted as a jack-o'-lantern with the words "Hayli's first Halloween costume."

Hayli joins her big sisters Kensli, 11, and Marli, 7, who Chance shares with his ex-wife Kirsten Corley. The two separated in 2024 and finalized their divorce in January 2025, and they've kept co-parenting their girls.

Congratulations to Chance and the family.
