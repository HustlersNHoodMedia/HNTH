Mark Fletcher Jr.'s mama doesn't fly. So when her son went to Miami, Linda Fletcher started driving to every game he played. Last season that added up to about 17,000 miles.

She kept making that drive after his father, Mark Sr., passed in October 2024. Mark says his dad is his why. His mama is the one who showed up every single week.

This week he surprised her with a brand new 2026 Cadillac Escalade.

"This is what every kid dreams of, being able to get their mom that car or that house," he said. "All the hard work she did for me my whole life, she took care of me and my siblings."

Her answer: "My son blessed me with this beautiful Cadillac."
