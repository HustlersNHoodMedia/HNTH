Cardi B was in Los Angeles when the FAA ground stop hit, and flights across the country started getting canceled.

The problem was the date. Her son Grey was about to turn one, and his party was waiting for her back home on the East Coast.

She posted about being stuck and trying to figure out how to get home. "Something feels very eerie right now in America," she said, and joked that she was ready to click her heels like Dorothy.

She found a way. Cardi flew across the country and made it back in time for "Grey's World," a space themed first birthday with balloons shaped like planets, and his big sisters Kulture and Blossom and his brother Wave right there with him.
