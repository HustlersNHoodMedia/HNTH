Druski got turned down by Netflix, Amazon and FX.

He was already making real money from brand deals by then. So instead of buying another car, another house or more jewelry, he bet it on himself. He put millions of his own money into producing Coulda Been Love, Coulda Been House and Coulda Been Records, and dropped them on YouTube.

Forbes put him on its top creators list this year at an estimated $20 million.

Now he says the companies want to buy the shows. "It's a bidding war."

In his own words: "If you don't see the vision yourself, you're almost doing yourself a disservice. It has to start with you."
