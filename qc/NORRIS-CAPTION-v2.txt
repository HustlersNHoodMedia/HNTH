Nafees Norris grew up on Estaugh Street off 20th and Tioga. He wanted to be a scientist at seven and let it go, because he never saw one who looked like him. He says he only went to college to stay out of trouble.

When they made him pick a major he asked what the hardest thing they had was. They told him nursing or biology. He took biology.

His family got put out of their house on that same block. In 2020 he became the first African American accepted into Thomas Jefferson University's Biopharmaceutical Process Engineering master's program, and he did not celebrate. His words: I got in, but I got to graduate. I got to set the bar.

His father made suits for all his sons for prom. Nafees never went to his. So when he walked at graduation, his father pulled a suit he sewed in the 1970s out of his own closet and cut it down to his son's size.

Then he left the lab job in Delaware and started Nafees Innovations, putting North Philly kids in real lab coats with real equipment.
