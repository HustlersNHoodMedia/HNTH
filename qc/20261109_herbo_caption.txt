G Herbo lost his father two years ago. Friday night at the United Center in Chicago, his jeweler walked on stage in the middle of his set and put a chain around his neck.

The pendant is a pink-diamond medallion. Carved into the front is the photo of his dad holding him the day he was born. In the back, sealed inside, are his father's ashes.

Backstage he kept it simple. "I know he would have been here. So I got him in. The ashes in the back."
