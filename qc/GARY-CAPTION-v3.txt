The storm hit Gary on August 11 with winds near 100 mph. More than 370,000 customers lost power. Almost two weeks later 8,600 homes were still sitting in the dark, everything in the fridge gone.

Trae Tha Truth drove up from Houston with Relief Gang. Chainsaws first, cutting trees off houses and off the lines. Then an 18 wheeler of water, diapers, formula and cleaning supplies, and hot plates handed out on the corners the trucks never reached. Seniors who could not walk to a distribution center got theirs carried to the door.

Gary is Michael Jackson's hometown. It is also 76 percent Black, and Freddie Gibbs, who grew up there, said the part out loud: this did not start with the storm.
