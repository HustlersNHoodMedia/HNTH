Janai told her two daughters they were going to look at a house. Pink dresses, little bows, blindfolds on for the surprise. They walked into an empty living room with balloons that spelled HOME, and the blindfolds came off.

The house was already hers. Brand new build in Atlanta, closed and done.

Her realtor Jasmine says Janai had been in her DMs for years talking about buying a home. Years of working, saving and staying on it while raising two girls. When it finally happened she did not settle either. Over $50,000 in seller paid incentives, a 3.75 percent rate, every appliance included.

"She worked, she sacrificed, she stayed focused and made it happen for her babies. They thought they were just coming to see a house. Whole time mama bought them a home."

Pink confetti, hugs in an empty house, and two little girls who now have their own rooms.
