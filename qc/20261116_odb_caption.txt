Wu-Tang Clan officially went into the Rock & Roll Hall of Fame this weekend in Los Angeles, and Ol' Dirty Bastard was there through his family.

ODB passed on November 13, 2004, two days before his 36th birthday. The induction landed 22 years later, the night before he would have turned 58.

His son, Young Dirty Bastard, accepted with RZA, GZA, Method Man, Raekwon, Ghostface Killah, Inspectah Deck, U-God, Masta Killa and Cappadonna, and the family walked the carpet with him. The Clan closed the night with "Shimmy Shimmy Ya" for Dirty.

Method Man said it on that stage: "We went from being banned on the radio to the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame."
