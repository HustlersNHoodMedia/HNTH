Rehan Staton and his older brother Reggie grew up in Maryland with a father who worked two and three jobs at a time. Some nights the lights were off anyway. Both brothers hauled trash to help him.

When Rehan got his shot at college, Reggie dropped out of his own and worked at the trash company so the family could afford it. Rehan cleaned dumpsters from 4 to 7 in the morning, then went to class.

In 2020 he got into Harvard Law, and Tyler Perry offered to pay his tuition. In 2023 he graduated.

"I couldn't have done it alone," he said.
