Shauntelle Jones and her five kids had nowhere stable to live. To stay together, they slept in empty houses in Detroit.

The first time she asked for help, it was just her and her oldest son at the door of COTS. "I was in a bad place. I just knocked on the door. I was like, 'here I am.'"

Years later she was homeless again. This time she stayed with the program long enough to do something she never got to do. She went back and finished high school, cap and gown and all.

Her kids used to ask her where they were sleeping that night. Now they come home and go to bed, and they're running track, boxing, cheering and playing soccer.

"My oldest son said, 'Ma, we're not homeless.' They used to ask where we wanna sleep at. They can go climb in that bed without no problems now."

She says without that help she'd probably still be homeless, and maybe without her children.
