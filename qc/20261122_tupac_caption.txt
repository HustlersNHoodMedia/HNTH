In 1994, Tupac sat down with Ed Gordon on BET and talked about why he lived the way he did.

He said he went to school all the way and was ready for college. All the kids around him were filling out applications. The only thing that stopped him was money.

"I'm not thuggin' for me, I'm thuggin' for my family. I pay all the bills. I feed my whole family."
