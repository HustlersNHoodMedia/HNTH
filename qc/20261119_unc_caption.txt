Derrick Moore grew up in Meridian, Mississippi, and his family started out in public housing.

He worked in a factory and sold plates of food to his coworkers on the side. That turned into a hot dog cart, then a kitchen, then a barbecue spot in Sandusky, Ohio, named after his son, Derrick Jr.

When the pandemic hit, his restaurants didn't make it. The food truck he tried after that didn't last either.

Back when he was still running his own place, he filmed daily videos inviting people to come eat, and he prayed somebody would do the same for him.

So he became that person. Today he's "Unc" to more than 189,000 followers on TikTok, pulling up to family spots, food trucks and corner counters across Northeast Ohio and telling his nieces and nephews where to eat. A lot of those reviews he does for free.

This week he spent the day at Superior Grill & Chicken in Cleveland helping them give away Polish Boys, with the whole store packed behind him.

"I don't wake up mad. I wake up with the intent to do good," he said.
