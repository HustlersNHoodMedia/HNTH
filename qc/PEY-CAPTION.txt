An 11-year-old from the Mississippi Gulf Coast spent six months in a children's hospital in New Orleans. A rare mutation had his own immune system attacking his red blood cells, and the only cure was a bone marrow transplant.

From that bed he kept saying the same thing. "I just want to be a child again. I want to go to school."

His big brother Acie turned out to be his match, and he gave him his stem cells.

On October 1st, one year after the transplant, he came back to the hospital in a blue suit and a red bow tie to ring the bell. His mama and his grandma were in the room. Then a brass band started playing, and he danced out with a second-line umbrella.
