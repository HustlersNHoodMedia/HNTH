Jon Wess-Pass is 11 and in fourth grade in Holly Springs, Mississippi.

On October 22 he saw a 4-year-old girl get hit by a car. He called 911. The operator asked if she was breathing and he said, "She's breathing!"

Then he took off his jacket and laid it on the ground for her. He put his lunchbox under her head. She kept trying to close her eyes, and he kept telling her, "Please don't go to sleep."

He had learned what to do in an emergency at school one week earlier.

The girl had cuts and bruises but no broken bones. Her mama, Jada Jackson, called it a miracle. "He did what he was supposed to do," she said.

This week the police chief and the mayor held an assembly in front of his whole school to honor him. "He's a role model for all the kids in this city," the chief said.
