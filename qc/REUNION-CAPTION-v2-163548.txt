For almost 20 years, Will only saw his father on FaceTime. His wife had never met the man in person.

Last week his baby sister, who he also hadn't seen in person in almost 20 years, flew in to spend a few days with him. What Will didn't know was that their dad was coming too. He rode in on the train, got there the same day, and waited until the next morning.

Will was asleep on the couch. His dad walked in, leaned over and woke him up.

Will sat up and just stared at him. Then he stood up and they held each other for a minute and a half. When they finally let go, he sat back down and covered his face.

"Our house has been full of love, dance and heart to hearts all day."
