Dion Dawson got about an hour's notice. Then Barack and Michelle Obama walked into his building on the South Side of Chicago the day before Thanksgiving. "Put us to work," the former president told them, and for about 25 minutes the two of them packed boxes of fresh produce next to his team.

Dion is a Navy veteran from Englewood who has been homeless himself, as a kid and again when he came home from the service. In 2020 he started with one refrigerator on the block, stocked every day for his neighbors. Today Dion's Chicago Dream delivers fresh food to 5,400 homes every week, free.

He says the families getting those boxes this week won't even know who packed them.
