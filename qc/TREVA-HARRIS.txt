Treva Harris turned a lot on Lancaster Avenue into a castle for her daughter's prom in 2024. Twelve hours of setup, $27,000, and she filmed the whole thing for Instagram. Called it the home of the Philly Princess.

Her bio still reads the same way. Multi-industry empire builder. Childcare, healthcare, real estate, logistics. I find the money.

Here is how she was finding it.

April 2020 she applied for a disaster loan for her Philadelphia childcare center and put down three employees. Two months later she went back for a PPP loan and told them the same business had 27 employees and a monthly payroll of $214,000. She sent fabricated IRS paperwork with it. They gave her $535,000.

Prosecutors say it went to luxury retail, large cash withdrawals and checks written to her boyfriend's business.

Friday a federal judge gave her a year and a day, three years of supervision, and ordered every dollar of the $535,000 back.

Nobody ever said the prom money came out of that loan.
