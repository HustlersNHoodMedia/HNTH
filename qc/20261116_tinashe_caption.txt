Tinashe just got her first Grammy nominations, and she got two.

"Too Easy" is up for Best Dance Pop Recording and "Melatonin" for Best Dance/Electronic Recording, both from Popstar, the album that gave her the first top 10 of her career when it debuted at No. 6 on the Billboard 200.

Just last month, on the In Your Dreams podcast, she had made peace with never getting the call: "I've never even been nominated. It's okay. I gave it up. It's fine. So, now I've moved on to family and children."

When the nominations dropped she brought back an old post of hers from March 2021 and added two words: "Omg finallyyyyyy."
