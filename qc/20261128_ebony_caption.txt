A single mom in Nevada put a red bow on a car for her 16-year-old son.

She's raising three boys and working three jobs so they don't go without. Her son is holding it down too: straight A's, a job of his own, and he still shows up in his mama's skits.

She blindfolded him in the driveway, told him how proud she was, then pulled it off. He hugged her and went straight for the driver's seat.

Her man stepped up and helped make it happen, for kids he didn't make.

"I work hard, you work hard. We help each other as a family."
