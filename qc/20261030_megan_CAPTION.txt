Megan Thee Stallion's mom, Holly Thomas, passed in March 2019, right when her daughter's music was starting to take off.

Holly was a rapper herself, and she was the one managing Megan in the beginning.

Megan was already taking classes at Texas Southern University in Houston, and even with the tours, the hits and three Grammys, she kept going.

"I want to get my degree because I really want my mom to be proud," she said. "She saw me going to school before she passed."

On December 11, 2021, she walked across the stage at Texas Southern and got her bachelor's degree in health administration.
