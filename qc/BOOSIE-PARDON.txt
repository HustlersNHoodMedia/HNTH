Boosie went on 60 Minutes Sunday night and told Lesley Stahl he paid $600,000 up front for a presidential pardon.

The deal was with Jack Burkman and Jacob Wohl. Contract dated September 25 of last year, on their letterhead, addressed to Torence Hatch. Consulting, lobbying, pardon strategy. Six hundred thousand.

They sent his lawyer texts with a list of people who were supposedly backing him. Laura Loomer. Erika Kirk. Speaker Mike Johnson. An aide who would walk the paperwork to the president herself.

All four say they never had anything to do with it. Loomer said her name was being used for clout without her permission, and that she thinks Boosie got scammed.

The contract had a clause. No pardon by January 31, half the money comes back. The pardon never came. They kept all of it and say the contract isn't valid.

"I just thought, this is the way it's done. I'm paying these guys, and they're gonna make it happen."

He's in arbitration now trying to get $300,000 of it back.
