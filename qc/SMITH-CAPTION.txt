Za'Darius Smith walked away from the Philadelphia Eagles five games into last season and never told anybody why.

He got a call from his mother's doctor saying she didn't have much longer. Retiring was the only way the team would let him leave, so he retired.

She passed. Then his grandmother. Then his father.

Ten months later he signed a one year deal with Atlanta, the team his mother always wanted for him because it is the short ride from home in Montgomery.

"I wasn't going to tell the story, but I'm going to tell it."
