Tamara Tunie has been on TV since 1987. Most of us know her as Dr. Melinda Warner, the medical examiner on Law & Order: SVU.

Last night at the Daytime Emmys she won her first Emmy ever, at 67, for playing Anita Dupree on Beyond the Gates, the first soap with a mostly Black cast in over 30 years.

Her mama, Evelyn Tunie, was in the audience. She is 92, and she crossed the country to be there.

"There is one person who has been on this journey with me every single step of the way, who has traveled across states, countries and oceans to support my dream," Tamara said. "I would not be who I am as an actor or a woman without her."

Then she held up the Emmy. "I love you, Mommy. This is for you."
