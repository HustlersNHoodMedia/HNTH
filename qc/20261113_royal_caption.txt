Lil' Kim lit the Empire State Building pink last night for the 30th anniversary of Hard Core. Her daughter Royal Reign came with her, and she didn't come to stand around.

The 12-year-old pulled up in a fur coat and shades and hit her mom's 'Crush On You' routine right there at the tower, step for step. She posted it herself as "crush on you 2.0."

Kim dropped Hard Core on November 12, 1996. Her daughter was born 18 years after that album came out, and she knows the choreography by heart.

The Queen Bee raised a bee.
