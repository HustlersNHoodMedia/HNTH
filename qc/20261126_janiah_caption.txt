JaNiah Smith is a high school senior in Detroit. After a man passed away outside her school, she and her classmates put together a telethon to raise money for people living on the street.

While she was doing it, she shared something about herself. Her own family had been homeless too.

They had bought a house from the city land bank for $1,000. The porch was coming down, the floor was caving in and the foundation couldn't be saved.

This week the Detroit Rescue Mission handed her family the keys to a remodeled home, furnished, with the kitchen stocked and a bedroom made just for her. Two years rent free, with the chance to buy it.

"I don't even know what to say," her mama said. "This is perfect."
