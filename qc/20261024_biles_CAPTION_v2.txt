Simone Biles and her little sister Adria were in foster care when their grandfather Ron Biles and his wife Nellie adopted them both. For as long as Simone can remember, they've been mom and dad.

At the 2016 Olympics in Rio, an NBC announcer called them her "grandfather and his wife" on air, then posted that they "may be mom and dad but they are NOT her parents."

People came for him, and he ended up apologizing.

Simone went out and won 4 gold medals in Rio with Ron and Nellie in the stands.
