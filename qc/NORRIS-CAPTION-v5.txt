Nafees Norris' father dressed every one of his sons for prom. He sewed the suits himself. Nafees is the one who never got to go.

He wanted to be a scientist at seven, and let it go because he never saw one who looked like him. He says he only went to college to stay out of trouble. When they made him pick a major he asked what the hardest thing they had was. They said nursing or biology. He took biology.

His family got put out of their house on that same block. In 2020 he became the first African American accepted into Thomas Jefferson University's Biopharmaceutical Process Engineering master's program, and he did not celebrate. His words: I got in, but I got to graduate. I got to set the bar.

So when the day finally came, his father went into his own closet, pulled out a suit he sewed in the 1970s and wore himself, and cut it down to his son's size.

Then he left the lab job in Delaware and started Nafees Innovations, putting North Philly kids in real lab coats with real equipment.
