Before Venus and Serena were born, Richard Williams was watching tennis on TV when commentator Bud Collins talked about the prize money a player had just won.

"$40,000 is not bad for four days' work," Collins said.

Richard made up his mind right there. He wrote out a plan, and when the girls were about four and a half he started taking them to the public courts in Compton.

Serena went on to win 23 Grand Slam singles titles. Venus won 7. Both made it to No. 1 in the world.

"I'm going to have me two kids and put them in tennis."
