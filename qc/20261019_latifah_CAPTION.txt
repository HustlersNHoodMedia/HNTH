Queen Latifah bought her big brother Lance a Kawasaki motorcycle for his birthday. Lancelot "Winki" Owens Jr. was an East Orange police officer, and he was her heart.

In 1992, while he was off duty, the bike crashed into a car. He never made it home.

She kept the key. She had it coated in gold and wore it on a chain around her neck for years, right through Living Single. Most people watching Khadijah James never knew what that key was.

She dedicated her album Black Reign to him, with a track called "Winki's Theme."

"There's nothing closer to me than my brother. That was my foundation."
