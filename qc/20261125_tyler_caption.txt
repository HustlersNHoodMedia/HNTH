Tyler Perry got stopped outside his studio in Atlanta by the kid from School of Hard Knocks, who asked how he got rich. He said he grew up broke in New Orleans and at one point was sleeping in his car. Today Forbes puts his fortune at $1.4 billion.

Then he explained why he never let go of his movies. "I watched my father build houses," he said. His father would come home happy with $800, and the man who owned the house would sell it and make $80,000.

"So even as a kid, I realized that ownership was important. You've got to own it if you can."
