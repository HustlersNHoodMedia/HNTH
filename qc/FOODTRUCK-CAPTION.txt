She was home in bed. Epilepsy, a run of seizures, the first stretch in a long time she hadn't been out on the lot. 🔒

Njeri Thompson runs the Mac Queen food truck on Jonesboro Road in Atlanta. Single mother, four daughters, and the four of them work it together. Her words: rarely do we ever leave the trailer sitting here, because my daughters and I, we're out here working.

August 11, middle of the afternoon. Somebody got through all six locks, hitched the trailer to a black Dodge Ram and pulled off the lot. Neighbors watched it happen and didn't think twice about it, because who steals a food truck at two in the afternoon. 🚛

She pulled in and didn't even put the car in park. Just started yelling, where is it, where is it.

They didn't take the trailer and leave. They stripped it. No wheel chocks. No stand. Not a screw, not a nut, not a bolt. 🔩

"They knew what they were doing."

That truck is the whole family's income. One of her daughters is at LSU. Right now they're working out whether she goes back this semester. 🎓

Her daughter Zhinnia, on camera: I've always seen her go and get it for us, always figure it out. We always have had a roof over our head.

Flock cameras caught the trailer heading down Jonesboro Road. Atlanta police are working the case.
