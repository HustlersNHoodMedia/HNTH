Kehlani's mom went into labor and got arrested at the hospital. Kehlani was born, and her mom left that hospital in custody.

Kehlani got raised by her aunt. She grew up on visits, taking pictures with her mama inside, hearing she was "on vacation" or "at work."

Now Kehlani is executive producing Live From The Hole, and the first episode was filmed inside the women's prison in Chino, California. The same prison her mom did time in. She sat in a circle with the mothers locked up there and told them: "Some of you guys know my mom more than I know my mom."

In that same prison, her mom gave birth to Kehlani's little brother cuffed to a gurney. Kehlani says it's a story she had never told.

She walked out with a different picture of the woman she grew up angry at. "My mom probably wanted to raise me. She was just dealt a really crazy hand."
