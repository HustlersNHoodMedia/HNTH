Crystal Mason went to vote in 2016. Her name wasn't on the list at her polling place in Tarrant County, Texas, so she filled out a provisional ballot. That ballot was never counted.

She was on supervised release at the time for a federal tax case, and she has always said nobody told her that meant she couldn't vote. In 2018 Texas sentenced her to five years in prison for it.

The case sent her back to federal prison for 10 months. Her daughter kept the house running. Her son gave up a college football scholarship to help.

In 2024 an appeals court threw out the conviction, and the Tarrant County DA appealed to keep it alive. This week Texas' highest criminal court let the acquittal stand. There is nothing left to appeal.

Mason says the DA's wife recently knocked on her door asking her to support his reelection.

"In all these years, I never lost my faith that justice would be done," she said. "I am so happy my nightmare is over and I can move on."
