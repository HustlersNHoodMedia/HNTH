Moneybagg Yo fed 200 families back home in Memphis this week.

His Bagg's Table giveaway on Monday handed out turkeys, fresh produce and full bags of groceries at Liberty Park ahead of Thanksgiving.

Bagg grew up in South Memphis, raised by a single mom. He left high school in his senior year to help her feed his little sisters, and put whatever was left into studio time.

"No matter where I go, I always want to make sure I'm giving back to the people and the city that helped make me," he wrote.
