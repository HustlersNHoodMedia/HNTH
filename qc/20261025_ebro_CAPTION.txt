Ebro Ahmed was standing outside the store where he works in St. Paul, Minnesota when a school bus rolled past him with the door wide open.

He looked in and saw the driver having a seizure behind the wheel. There were 18 middle school kids on that bus.

He ran after it and jumped on. He has no medical training, and in the rush he threw it in reverse by accident before he got it into neutral and pulled the parking brake. Then he called the police and an ambulance for the driver.

Nobody got hurt. The driver is home resting, and the kids were checked on at school the next day.

Three days later, a local comedian pulled up to the store with a Superman and a $1,000 check made out "for being a real life superhero." He's 27, and he says he's putting it back into his community.

"I really felt like it was my duty."
