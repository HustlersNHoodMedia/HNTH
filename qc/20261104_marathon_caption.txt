James Thomas ran his first New York City Marathon in 1980.

On Sunday he ran his 43rd. He's 78 now. He started a little after 9 in the morning and crossed the finish line in Central Park around 11:19 at night, almost 14 hours later. He was the last one in.

The crowd and the race staff were still there waiting for him when he got there.

After he crossed, he said this one was his last. His mind still wants to keep going. His body can't do it anymore.
