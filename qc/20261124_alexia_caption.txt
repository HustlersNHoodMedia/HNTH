Alexia Jayy won The Voice in April. Last Friday she was moving things out of her house in Mobile, Alabama, getting ready to move, and she lay down for a quick nap.

Her son called her from her grandmother's house. He wanted to show her what he had learned to cook, so she got up and went to see him.

"Less than six minutes after leaving, we received the call that our house was on fire," she wrote.

When she looked at the spot where she was going to take that nap, the ceiling had caught fire and collapsed on it. "God is so good," she wrote.

The house is still standing, but the family lost most of what was inside. Clothes, shoes, beds, furniture, appliances. Nobody was hurt, and now they're starting over and asking for help with the basics.

"That fire took STUFF away... but not our lives," she posted on Facebook.
