Xavier Leathers is 12 years old. Early Saturday morning in Prince George's County, Maryland, he was asleep on the couch when he heard his mother's boyfriend come in and start choking her.

He said something in his body told him he had to move right then. The man got two good hits on him before he could get past to the kitchen. He went anyway, grabbed the sharpest knife he could find, came back and stabbed him twice.

His mama didn't know any of that while it was happening. All she knew was that the man on top of her suddenly let go. She found out why when she overheard the police saying he had been stabbed, and her first thought was that her son was in trouble. They told her he was fine. He was defending her.

The man was taken into custody. She went to the hospital, then filed for a protective order.

"The police told me I did a good job. I saved my mother's life, and I should never have been put in that situation to begin with."
