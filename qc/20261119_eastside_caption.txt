Olajuwon Ajanaku grew up on the Eastside of Atlanta, swinging his first golf clubs at John A. White and going to Cedar Grove High.

Back then, school shopping meant Lenox. Phipps Plaza, the mall across the street, was out of reach.

After Morehouse, he and fellow Morehouse man Earl Cooper started Eastside Golf in 2019, a golf brand built on where they came from. They kept building. The brand now has 15 collaborations with Nike, a store at the Detroit airport and racks at Nordstrom in New York.

Now Eastside Golf has opened its first flagship store, right inside Phipps Plaza.

"Before there was a store in Phipps Plaza, there was a kid from the Eastside with a golf club, a vision, and examples all around him showing him that being yourself could take you somewhere," he wrote.

He calls it a full-circle moment, and says it isn't the finish line.
