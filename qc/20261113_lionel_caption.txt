Lionel Richie walked out at the Wynn in Las Vegas this week and told the crowd, "I don't know where I was, but I'm back."

It had been a rough stretch. In June he got dizzy in the middle of opening night of his tour with Earth, Wind & Fire and had to leave the stage. In August a show in St. Louis ended with three days in the hospital. In September doctors treated his heart for an irregular rhythm, and he had to put shows on hold while he recovered.

Two months later, at 77, he was back at his Vegas residency like he never left, joking that he drinks electrolytes for breakfast, lunch and dinner now, and thanking the room for caring about him.
