Michael Jackson's "Thriller" album sat at No. 1 for 37 weeks. The song itself never got there. The single peaked at No. 4 on the Billboard Hot 100 in 1984, and that was as high as it went.

Now his oldest son Prince wants to fix that.

He's calling it the Thriller Challenge. From October 30 to November 5, he's asking everybody to buy the song, stream it as much as they can and call their local radio station to request it, so it all counts in the same chart week.

"The goal is to try and get my father's song 'Thriller,' the single, to number one," Prince said. "We are trying to get my father another number one."

You in?
