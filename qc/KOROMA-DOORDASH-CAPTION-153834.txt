William Koroma was delivering a DoorDash order in Bowie, Maryland, when his brakes gave out. He hit another car. His was done: front end crushed, ambulances on the scene.

The customer, Glenn, got a phone call. William was apologizing. He said the food was fine, it was sitting on the sidewalk, and if Glenn wanted it he could come get it.

Glenn filmed himself driving over, feeling terrible about picking up chicken from a crash site. Then he met William, who had just lost his car, his job and picked up medical bills in one night, and was still worried about somebody else's dinner.

The video did millions of views. Glenn set up a GoFundMe. Cash App froze William's account because too many people were sending money at once.

This week William pulled up to Glenn's house in a white SUV. Paid for by strangers. He wanted to say thank you.

"This happened for a reason."
