Early Sunday morning, somebody opened fire at a homecoming block party in Vienna, Georgia. Two young men died at the scene. Dozens more were hurt.

Twenty-six of them were brought to Crisp Regional Hospital, nine miles away. One doctor and five nurses were working the emergency room. In a normal month they see two or three gunshot patients. That night they got 18 in about an hour.

People who weren't on call got the phone call at 2 a.m. and came in. Doctors, nurses, administrators, even housekeeping.

All 26 patients who came through their doors survived.

"They got a call at 2 o'clock in the morning and showed up," surgeon Vince Culpepper said. "If they hadn't, it would have been a whole different story."

Rest easy Ja'Quavion Jackson and Macaleb Jackson.
