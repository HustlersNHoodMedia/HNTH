Quentin Good is 18, a senior who plays basketball for his high school in Clarksville, Tennessee. This week his mama was at the hospital with one of his brothers when a fire started on the front porch.

His 10-year-old brother saw the smoke first. Quentin got his seven siblings, his grandmother and the pets out of the house. Then he went back and dragged two burning chairs away from the porch so the house wouldn't go with it.

He was airlifted with third-degree burns on his arms, feet and face. He wants to be back on the court before the season ends.

"Because of his quick action, he saved our home," his mama said.
