Thursday night a storm came through Kettering, Maryland, and around midnight Andre Carter watched the tree in his front yard start tipping toward his house. He ran upstairs. His wife was screaming. His 12 year old was in bed under the roof, the ceiling and a beam. Carter could hear him screaming but could not see him.

He tried to lift the beam off his son's stomach. He couldn't. So he held it up, kept his hands on it, and kept talking. "Just keep him calm, keep him talking. Help is coming soon for you." Close to twenty minutes like that, by his count, until Prince George's County firefighters got there and cleared the beam off the boy by inches.

The boy went to Children's National as a precaution and was out by morning, bruised and sore, nothing broken. Schools in the county were closed the next day for storm damage.

"I'm sure it will be a little bit different going to sleep next time. He'll be OK. One day at a time."
