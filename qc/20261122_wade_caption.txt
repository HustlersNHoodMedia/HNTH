In 2010, Dwyane Wade went looking for his kids and couldn't find them. He later called it the most frightening moment of his life.

He was already fighting his ex-wife in court in Chicago. After that, he went for full custody. His lawyer said the trial was one of the longest in Cook County history.

In March 2011 the judge ruled in a 102-page decision. She wrote that the mother had shown an "unstoppable and relentless" pattern of trying to keep the kids away from their father, and she gave Wade full custody. Zaire and Zaya flew to Miami that same day.

The next day his Heat teammates gave him a standing ovation in the locker room.

"You fight until you can't fight any more," he said.
