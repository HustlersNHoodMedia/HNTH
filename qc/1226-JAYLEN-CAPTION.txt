Jaylen Brown was out in the crowd in Boston last week when a mother caught his eye. Pregnant, three boys at home, one on the way. She had just walked her kids over to see him that morning. She told him she takes them to the Boys and Girls Club all the time, and that the shoes he gave her students meant something.

Then the question. "You guys have a car though?"

"We need to get one. I'm definitely going to need it now. I'm a single mom."

He asked if she trusted him. She said she did. He had her blindfolded, walked her across the park with her son holding her hand, and told her to open her eyes in front of a brand new car with a bow on it. Confetti. "Oh my God." She cried. Her boy did not let go of her hand the whole time.

The car came through a local dealership. The moment came from the man who took the time to ask a stranger what she needed.
