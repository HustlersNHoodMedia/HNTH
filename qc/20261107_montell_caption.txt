When Montell Jordan's prostate cancer came back last year, the plan was proton therapy. 37 treatments, five mornings a week for seven and a half weeks at Emory in Atlanta.

His wife Kristin knew they were going to need help. So a friend of theirs reached out to Q Parker of 112 and asked if he could handle one day.

On the first morning, Montell walked into the hospital and Q was already there. He told Montell he wasn't coming once. He was coming every day.

For 32 or 33 of those 37 mornings, Q drove an hour across Atlanta, leaving around 5 or 6 a.m., so he could be there for Montell and for Kristin while he went through it.

On Christmas Day, Montell found out the cancer was undetectable.

Telling the story to Tank and J. Valentine, he kept coming back to one thing: "That man didn't have to do that."
