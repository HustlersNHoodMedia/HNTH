Ice Cube was one of the producers on Straight Outta Compton, the movie about N.W.A. His son O'Shea Jackson Jr. wanted to play him.

Cube didn't just hand it over. O'Shea had to audition like every other actor, and it took about two years with director F. Gary Gray before the part was his.

"There was two years of auditioning with Gary," O'Shea said.

He ended up playing his own father in one of the biggest music movies ever made.
