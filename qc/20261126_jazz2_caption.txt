Jazz was at the store with her kids when a stranger asked if she had dropped a $20 bill. She told him it wasn't hers.

That stranger was Jimmy Darts. He gave her $500 for being honest, and that's when she told him the rest. She had been let go from her job. She had a two-month-old baby. They were sleeping in the car at a park, and the kids did their homework on the library wifi. One of her boys tried to hand Jimmy his only dollar.

In less than 24 hours, more than 18,000 people gave $400,000. He came back with the money and a new car, and then her family sat down to Thanksgiving dinner.

"It's going to be ok, mom," her son told her.
