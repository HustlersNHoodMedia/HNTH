A man went to his late stepdad's job to pick up his things and opened the old lunchbox. Three photos of him were taped inside the lid, one from his college graduation.

Ray worked nights at a machine shop and barely talked. He married the man's mama when the boy was around 8, and the stepson always figured everything Ray did for him was really for her.

A coworker told him Ray opened that lunchbox at lunch and asked everybody, "You wanna hear what my son did?"

He told the story in a Reddit thread.
