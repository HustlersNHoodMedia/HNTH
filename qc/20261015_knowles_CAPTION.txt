Back in 1999, the money from Destiny's Child's first single helped build a gym and youth center at St. John's, the church in downtown Houston where Beyoncé and Kelly Rowland grew up. They named it after the two of them.

Hurricane Harvey tore it up in 2017. A fire finished it off in 2021. For years it just sat there holding boxes.

This week it opened back up as 31 apartments for people who had been living on the street for a year or more, 16 of them built for residents who use a wheelchair or can't see or hear well. Counseling and case managers are right there in the building.

Most of the $8.4 million rebuild came from county recovery money. Beyoncé, Kelly and Mama Tina all came home for the opening, with Blue and Rumi too, and Beyoncé told the room she was baptized in that church.
