Natasha Howard just put her life in a book, and the part that hits hardest is her father.

He played pro basketball overseas while she was growing up in Toledo. The first time she remembers seeing him she was 10. She didn't tell him she had started playing, and he didn't ask. She didn't see him again until her senior year of high school, when she was the No. 2 recruit in the country and he wanted to be by her side for Senior Night.

"My entire life I had wondered why my father wasn't around, and here he was."

Her mama raised her alone. After her mom got out of a bad relationship they spent a few weeks in a shelter, and when money was tight they ate syrup on white bread. Her uncle Greg got her started by paying her $2 to dribble a ball. She spent it on candy and chips, and he came back every day to teach her.

She went No. 5 in the draft, and she has won 3 WNBA titles. Her book is "The Vision Through My Lens."
