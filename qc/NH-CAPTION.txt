Natasha Howard just put her life in a book, and it starts with her uncle Greg and $2.

She was a little girl in Toledo when he offered her two dollars to dribble a basketball. She spent it on candy and chips. He came back the next day, and the day after that, every day, to teach her the game, and he got a club coach to take a look at her.

Her mama raised her alone. After her mom got out of a bad relationship they spent a few weeks in a shelter, and when money was tight they ate syrup on white bread.

Her father played pro basketball overseas. The first time she remembers seeing him she was 10. She didn't tell him she played, and he didn't ask. He showed up again her senior year, when she was one of the top recruits in the country.

"My entire life I had wondered why my father wasn't around, and here he was."

She went No. 5 in the draft and has won 3 WNBA titles. The book is "The Vision Through My Lens."
