Demond Crump did 31 years as an entrepreneur before this one. Navy, car washes, concerts, real estate, and a move from New Orleans to Atlanta after Katrina with two days of clothes.

Then his wife Qiana had a partial hysterectomy and the bleeding afterward was heavy. He went looking for a pad that could handle it and couldn't find one in any big box store. So he had a prototype made. It cost $6 and took a year to get to market.

In 2019 the family launched Reign Premium Sanitary Napkins out of McDonough, Georgia. Today he runs it with his daughters Milan and Leah, and the whole family works it.

"As men, we understand the silent suffering that's been going on with these young girls and women right under our roof. We just didn't know."
