Sutton Jr. was 2 years old when he lost his father, Sutton Tennyson, in 2018.

He was actually due on his dad's birthday, November 5. He came early, on September 22.

This year, on what would have been his father's birthday, he asked his mom, Angela Simmons, for a balloon release. He picked out the cake himself, "Happy Birthday Dad" in gold letters, and chose white and gold balloons because, in his words, "my dad also really liked gold."

Before he let them go, he had one request for his father:

"Dad, please make sure you tell God what you want me to do when I'm older, and make sure God tells me."
