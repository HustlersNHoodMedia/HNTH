Chris Brown and Diamond Brown, the mother of his daughter Lovely, are back in court over money.

According to court documents, Diamond says Chris pays her about $7,000 a month and should be paying at least $30,000. She puts his net worth around $50 million, says she only has about $10,000 to her name, and subpoenaed his business managers to see his finances. They objected.

Chris already gave his side on his Instagram Story: "I LITERALLY PAY FOR EVERYTHING, her cars(2), her HOUSE, SCHOOLING, MEDICAL, and 10K a month." He also said he hasn't seen Lovely in 7 months.

The other mothers of his kids took his side, saying every one of his children is taken care of. Diamond answered them too.
