David Thurman has full custody of his three kids, and for about eight months home was a shelter hotel in New York.

When somebody asked what he needed, he didn't ask for money. "I'm really trying to get myself employment," he said. "I can't get a place without employment."

That same day his son was offered a brand new Spider-Man or $1,000 cash. He took the cash for his family.

A month later, 50 full-time job offers had come in. Then Michael Rapino, the CEO of Live Nation, saw the video and got David on FaceTime himself. "As a father of three, I felt for you, brother." He offered him a job with his team.

The community raised $100,000 and set him up with a financial advisor so it lasts. David walked his kids into their own apartment with a balcony, and when he said "God is good," one of them answered before he finished: "All the time."
