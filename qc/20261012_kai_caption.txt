A man driving Uber in Chicago didn't speak any English, so his passenger FaceTimed her 6-year-old daughter, Kai, and Kai translated for them in Chinese.

When he dropped her mom off, he got out of the car. He said he had to meet her in person.

Everybody in the comments asked the same thing: how?

Her mom answered. Kai has been learning Mandarin for three and a half years. Most of her school day is in Mandarin, she goes to another Chinese program after school until about 6:30, and she has private lessons on Saturdays. Her mom says the tuition is not cheap.

It was her dad's idea before she was even born.

"He always said, 'When we have another baby, she's going to learn Chinese!' And he stood on that!"
