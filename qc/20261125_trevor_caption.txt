Trevor Travier is 12. In October he was riding his bike in Michigan when a truck hit him and dragged him. He broke his arm and his elbow, and his hand was badly hurt.

One of the paramedics said Trevor kept telling him he was not dying that day, and answered everything with "yes sir."

He spent 39 days in the hospital and had eight surgeries. While he was there, Lions receiver Jameson Williams called him on FaceTime.

This week he came home. The firefighters, paramedics and officers from that day were in his yard with a Lions gift basket.

"My son is strong," his mama said. "I'm glad he's here."
