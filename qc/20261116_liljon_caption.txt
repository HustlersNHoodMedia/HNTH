Lil Jon is getting real about why his marriage to Nicole Smith ended after about 18 years.

On The School of Greatness he said he had to look in the mirror and admit he wasn't the greatest person in the marriage. And he says he missed what she was telling him the whole time: "I didn't see that my ex was trying to tell me, screaming out that she needed more love."

He also admitted he may not have been able to give her that love, and that all the time on the road touring is what kept the marriage going as long as it did. On Angie Martinez IRL he called her an extreme pessimist and said they could never get on the same page.

Accountability or too late? Where y'all stand on this one?
