Stevie Wonder's daughter Aisha was born in 1975, and he put her right inside a song. "Isn't She Lovely" came out on Songs in the Key of Life in 1976, and near the end of the track you can hear Stevie giving his baby girl a bath, with her babbling under his harmonica.

Aisha grew up and started singing backup for her father.

Now Stevie is on the 50th anniversary tour for that album, and Aisha is on stage with him. She walks him out and helps him move between his keyboards during the show. When the tour opened he even had a warning for any fan who got too friendly with her: "I've got eyes on you, baby."

The U.S. leg kicked off this week at Madison Square Garden.
