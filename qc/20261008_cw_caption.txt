Courtney Williams made $180,000 last WNBA season. She has been in the league since 2016, out of Folkston, Georgia.

This year the players held the line and won a new deal. In April, Courtney signed with the Minnesota Lynx for $2.4 million over two years.

If you watch Lynx games you already know her dad, Don. He is the man in the stands wearing her #10 jersey at home games. "I love seeing him up there turnt up," she told ESPN.

On a stream with her friends she said where the money went first. She bought her mama a house. And she bought him one too.

"I just bought my daddy a house."
