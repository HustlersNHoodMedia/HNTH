Leroy Russell has been cooking for 29 years. In 2024 he opened Spice of Life Jamaica in downtown Birmingham, right across the street from City Hall, and he says he put about $280,000 into it.

Everything is made from scratch, so the oxtails and the jerk chicken are cooked hours before anybody walks in.

When the city holds events at City Hall, the street gets barricaded and sometimes police are posted at his entrance. Customers get turned around. The food he already made goes in the trash, and he says that has added up to thousands and thousands of dollars.

He says he has been calling the city for three years and nobody has called him back. Earlier this month he went to the city council and said it out loud. A representative for the mayor said staff are working on a fix. He says nobody has reached out since.

He is not asking them to cancel anything. He just wants people to be able to walk through his door.

"You cannot block somebody from their business place."
