This one is from Jackson, Missouri, back in the spring of 2020, and it just went around again this week.

After a big storm, Hubble Creek in the city park was running high and kids were out playing in it. Kinsley Stuart, 5, was on the low water bridge with her mom. "I blinked and she was there. I blinked again and she was gone." The current had pulled her under the bridge into an open culvert.

Aiden Kyle, 11, was the one who noticed she was missing. He saw her hand holding the edge of the bridge, reached under and grabbed her wrist. "My first thought was there was no way I'm letting this girl go." Then the water started taking him too. Isaiah Randol, 15, jumped in behind him. Then Alex Niedbalski and Aiden's brother Eli, both 15, and the four of them pulled Kinsley and Aiden out.

She spent the night at the hospital and walked out the next morning without a scratch.

Kinsley's parents, Casey and Jesse, own Kenny's Flippin Burgers. Casey told the boys they eat there free, forever. "There is no amount of money that can ever be put on what you did."
