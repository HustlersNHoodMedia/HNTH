Every night around nine, Félix Cárdenas walks several blocks in Ñemby, Paraguay, to a bus stop with no lights on it. He stands there with his hands behind his back until his daughter's bus finally shows up, and then he walks her the rest of the way home.

He's 72. He's been doing it since 2019.

At first he used to pick Belén up right at her job. She told him she wasn't in school anymore, that she needed room to move on her own. So he compromised. He stopped going to her work and started waiting at the stop instead. He does the same for her brothers, and now for her son.

He retired from the phone company a decade ago and gave all that time back to his family. When the bus doesn't come, he drives her the hour into Asunción himself.

Belén took this photo in 2022 and put it online. People came for her, asking how she could let a man his age stand out there at night. Her answer was that it stresses him more not to, so she just gets home as fast as she can.

He told the local paper it isn't a favor. "Es mi obligación." It's my job to take care of them.
