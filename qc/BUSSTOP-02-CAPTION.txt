Félix Cárdenas is 72. Most nights he walks several blocks to a dark, empty bus stop in Ñemby, Paraguay, and waits there in the cold, the heat, the rain, so his daughter doesn't have to make the walk home by herself.

He'd been doing it since 2019 when his daughter Belén took the picture, back in 2022. When it's cold he brings her a coat. He used to pick her up straight from work until she asked for a little room to move on her own, so he compromised and started waiting at the stop instead. He does the same for her brothers, and now for her son.

He retired from the phone company ten years earlier and gave the time to his family. He learned to be careful the hard way, living in Argentina through the seventies. He told the local paper it isn't a favor: "Es mi obligación." It's my job to take care of them.

People in the comments came for Belén, asking how she could let a man his age stand outside at night. Her answer was that it stresses him more not to. So she just gets home as fast as she can.
