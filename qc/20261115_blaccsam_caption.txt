Blacc Sam says he and Nipsey Hussle used to work in a restaurant when they were younger. Back then the joke between the two brothers was always the same: "Man, when we get the money right, we need to get a restaurant."

Nipsey got the first taste of it. He did a collab with the Fatburger right next door to the Marathon store on Crenshaw and talked them into putting a Marathon Burger on the menu.

Today Sam runs Marathon Burger on Melrose, in Long Beach, Venice Beach, Canoga Park and at LAX. And a man who used to promote shows for Nipsey brought him a 50/50 deal on a spot in Times Square, in a building locked into a 30-year lease. Sam says it should open before New Year.
