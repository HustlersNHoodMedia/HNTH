Sterling Sharpe was the big brother, and for seven seasons in Green Bay he was one of the best receivers in football. Then a neck injury ended his career in 1994.

His little brother Shannon kept going. When the Broncos won Super Bowl XXXII, Shannon gave his first ring to Sterling. Years later Sterling called it "the most precious gift I've ever received."

In 2011 Shannon went into the Hall of Fame and joked that he was the only player in there who was the second-best in his own family.

In August 2025 Sterling finally got his gold jacket. In the middle of his speech he called Shannon up, took the jacket off and put it in his little brother's arms so he could be the only player in the Hall with two of them.
