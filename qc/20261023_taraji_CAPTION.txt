Taraji P. Henson grew up in Southeast DC and became a mama while she was still trying to make it as an actress.

When it was time to go chase it for real, she didn't leave her son behind.

"We raised $700, I packed my son up, and I moved to L.A.," she said.

Years later she was nominated for an Oscar for The Curious Case of Benjamin Button, and the whole world got to know her as Cookie on Empire.
