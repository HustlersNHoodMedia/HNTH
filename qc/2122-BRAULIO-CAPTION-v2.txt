Braulio is 87 years old and he still pushes an ice cream cart through his neighborhood in Chicago every day, out in the heat. He is from Iguala, Guerrero. In all these years he has never asked anybody for anything except the chance to keep working.

A neighbor ran into him on the sidewalk, bought a paleta off him for three dollars and could not get him out of his head. So he filmed him and put the video up. Millions of people saw it and the comments all said the same thing: let this man rest.

Then he went back out to find him. It took him almost an hour and a half of walking the blocks before he spotted Braulio sitting by himself in the park. He sat down with him, showed him the video on his phone and told him what strangers were trying to do for him.

"He never asked for fame or charity. He only ever asked for the chance to work. But now he deserves rest, dignity and peace."

He is 87. He is still out there pushing the cart.
