Za'Darius Smith got a call from his mother's doctor. She didn't have much longer.

He told the Eagles. They didn't want to let him go. Five games into his eleventh season, three Pro Bowls and 70 sacks behind him, he retired. That was the only way out he had.

His mama died. Then his grandmother died. Then his father died.

He said he was in a dark place and wasn't sure he'd play again. The Browns called to check on him and he told them he still loved the game. He worked out for Cleveland but the money wasn't right.

Then Atlanta called. He signed for one year.

He didn't pick the Falcons for the contract. He picked them because Atlanta is the closest team to Greenville, Alabama, where he's from, and his mama always wanted the family to be able to drive up and watch him play.

He had one year of high school football. He's about to start his twelfth in the NFL.

"I wasn't going to tell the story, but I'm going to tell it."
