Biggie said it on "Ten Crack Commandments": keep your family and business completely separated.

After he passed, his mother Voletta Wallace and his widow Faith Evans did the opposite. In 2007 they started a company together to run his music, his name and his image, and each one gave half of her share to Biggie's kids, T'yanna and CJ.

Ms. Wallace passed in February 2025. Weeks before, she changed her trust, took CJ off as a trustee and put Biggie's old manager in charge. After that, Faith stopped paying the mother's share. The judge wrote that she was "presumably angered by the affront to her son."

The judge opened her first ruling with Biggie's own line. Last week she put a number on it: $12.5M, close to $14M with interest. Faith's side had argued the trust was owed $48,612.
