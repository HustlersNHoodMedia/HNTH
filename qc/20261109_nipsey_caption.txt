Netflix and Disney's Onyx Collective were both at the table for Nipsey Hussle's docuseries, and according to his big brother Blacc Sam, the offers went past $20 million, up into the high 20s. The family turned every one of them down.

They want to tell Nip's whole life the way he lived it, not just how it ended and not just the fame. So the plan is to put all eight or nine episodes out for free, for the people who actually rode with him from Crenshaw.

Blacc Sam said it plain on The Breakfast Club: "There's no level of money that can be presented to have us walk away."
