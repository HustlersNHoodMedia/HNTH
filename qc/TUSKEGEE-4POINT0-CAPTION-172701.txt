Tuskegee University was built in 1881 in Alabama, with Booker T. Washington running it out of a one room church. Most of the first students were children of people who had been enslaved.

This week the school announced that ten members of the Class of 2026 finished all four years with a perfect 4.0. Not one grade below an A, in any class, for four years.

Joi Kayla Axem. Kerri Danielle Barnes. Nilajah Nyota Buchanan. Yasmin Aleksandria Davis. Alaynah Eldridge. Darren Raymond Hall. Keevion Hardamon. Charniece Elliana Jones. Brooklyn Monae Macklin. Leah D. Saunders.

"I cried thinking I am my ancestors' wildest dream," Alaynah Eldridge said.
