When Shaq left LSU for the NBA in 1992, he and his mama Lucille made a deal. "You do this now and go back to school later."

He kept his end. In December 2000 he skipped a Lakers game to walk at LSU and get his degree.

Then it was her turn. Lucille had raised four kids and never got a degree of her own, so she went back to college. Her son covered the tuition, with one condition.

"I promised him that I wouldn't waste his money and he said that as long as I got good grades, he would continue to pay my tuition."

She kept her grades up. She graduated from Bethune-Cookman with Shaq in the room, then went on and earned her master's in 2005.

"It took 30 years, but I never lost thirst for learning."
