On August 11 a derecho hit northwest Indiana with 99 mph winds. Three hundred thousand customers lost power, the biggest outage in NIPSCO's history.

Most of Indiana got the lights back in a few days. Gary didn't. Two weeks later, two thirds of everybody still in the dark in northwest Indiana lived there.

Keith Lee reviews restaurants on TikTok. He drove through Gary, filmed the trees down and the streetlights out, and posted it because nobody outside the city seemed to know it was still happening. Then he put up $15,000 of his own money.

Tyrese Haliburton saw the post and matched it.

August 24 at Roosevelt, they gave out 2,000 relief kits, one per household. Mops, brooms, disinfectant, work gloves, N95 masks, heavy trash bags. Not emergency food. Supplies to clean out your own house. They added back to school supplies because the kids were going back to class from homes with no electricity.

Thousands showed up. The line went down the block.

Four days later the governor ordered an investigation into the power company.
