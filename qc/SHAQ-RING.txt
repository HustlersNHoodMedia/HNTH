Shaq was in a Zales in McDonough, Georgia buying hoop earrings when he heard the kid next to him at the counter.

"He was just so shy, and he was saying, how much do I owe to pay off my ring?"

The kid had it on layaway. A little down this month, a little more next month. Shaq asked the clerk what the balance was, slid his card across without saying anything to anybody, and told him to tell his girlfriend it was handled.

The kid said no. Said he couldn't let him do that. Shaq told him not to worry about it, that he does it all the time, and shook his hand.

Somebody in the store filmed it and it got out. Shaq said that part bothered him. "I didn't mean for that to get out, because I don't do it for that."

When people ask him why he keeps doing this, he points at his parents. A man who made thirty thousand a year and a secretary who made twenty.
