She dropped out of high school and carried it for eighty years. 🎓

Helen Woodyard is from LaGrange, Georgia. She raised four kids and watched every one of them get a degree. Her words: I never finished, so I always regretted not getting my diploma. My four children finished, and I always wanted to do what they did.

So on her 88th birthday she walked into West Georgia Technical College and signed up for adult ed. Started from the beginning.

"When I came to class on my first day, it was like I had never left, even 80 years later."

Three days a week she drove herself to campus. Studied at night. Barely missed a class. 958 hours of coursework, and the two things that gave her the most trouble were math and the computer. She had to learn a scientific calculator at 88 to sit the exams. 💻

"I kept going because I don't quit anything that I start. The Lord started a work in me, and I was willing to spend all the time I had left before I died to finish."

July 29, she crossed the stage. Thirty-something family members in the room and the whole place on its feet. The next oldest person in Georgia to do this in the last ten years was 75. 🙌

She'll tell you straight that she didn't do it for a job. She did it because she wanted it.

Now she wants to teach other old folks to read and write.
