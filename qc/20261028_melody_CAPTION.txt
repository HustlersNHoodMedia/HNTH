Melody Clay was already raising four kids in Washington when her five nieces and nephews ended up in foster care, and she wasn't about to let the system split them up. In May 2025 she officially adopted all five.

"When I look at them, that's what keeps me going," she said. "They depend on me, I don't have the option of failing."

Now there are nine kids in the house, seven of them still under 18. On September 11 her landlord texted that the lease wouldn't be renewed because they needed the house back. Rent around Seattle was too high for a family that size, so she got approved for a five-bedroom house in Georgia, and then the transmission went out on her Suburban, the only car big enough to move them all.

She's working extra hours and put her pride aside to start a GoFundMe for the move.
