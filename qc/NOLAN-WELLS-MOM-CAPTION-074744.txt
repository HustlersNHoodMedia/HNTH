Nolan Wells went out on a boat with friends in July and never made it home. Two days later they found his body on Horn Island, off the coast of Mississippi. He was 18.

Tyler Perry paid for the funeral. Colin Kaepernick paid for an independent autopsy. Byron Allen put in $100,000. The independent pathologist found a blunt force injury from hours before he passed and could not say how he died. Last week a grand jury decided not to charge anyone.

Two days after that, his mother, Christine Wells-Wonsley, sat down with NBC News. The interviewer called her son a man. She stopped him.

"Let's not call him a man, because 18, you're still a teenager. I know by law you're an adult. Nolan was a boy. He was a child. He was our child. So for everyone watching, please stop referring to him as a man, because he was a teenager. He was 18 when he left this earth."

The investigation is still open.
