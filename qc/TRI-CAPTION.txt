Somebody pulled up next to RayShawn Adorjan and started filming out the car window. He had his youngest up on his shoulders and his oldest sitting in a milk crate on the back of the trike. That is the school run. He does not have a car.

He is 35 and raising both boys on his own, six and four. He paints houses for a living. Without a car he has to turn down jobs, because there is no way to carry his tools on a bike.

The clip passed 400,000 views in a couple of days. He reposted it himself and wrote four words under it: don't make no excuses.

His sister has started a fundraiser so he can finally get a vehicle for the three of them.

In his own words, about his sons: they are what makes my world spin.
