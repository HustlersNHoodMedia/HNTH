For nearly two years, a Netflix reality star built an audience of 34,000 documenting that exact program. Study nights. Clinical rotations. What's in my PA school bag. People were coming to her for advice on how to get in.

Duke Health, asked directly: they can find no record of her ever applying to or attending the program.

The woman who put the receipts out is Kiera Brown. First generation in her family to finish college, class of 2026, and the class historian, which means keeping the record of who was actually there was literally her job. She says she noticed during her first year and said nothing. She posted 40 straight weeks of her rotations, took no summer break, got hooded at Duke Chapel on August 7, and only then opened her mouth.

Her words: too busy actually earning the degree to comment.
