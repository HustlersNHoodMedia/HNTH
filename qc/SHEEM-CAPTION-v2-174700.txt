Sheem grew up on Section 8. His mama made a way for him when they didn't have much.

He became an ER nurse and bought her a house. He lives in Atlanta and works emergency room shifts in San Francisco, so every week he gets on a plane and flies 2,000 miles to go to work. More than 400 flights in three years. This summer he worked 30 days in a row.

Last week he posted the video. The SOLD sign in his hands. His mom in her kitchen holding up the keys. Then her walking across the front yard of a house that's hers, with the U-Haul parked out front.

"The same woman who made a way for me when we didn't have much, now she has her own," he wrote. "Becoming a nurse changed my life, but being able to change hers is the real blessing."
