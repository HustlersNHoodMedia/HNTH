Nevada Pierre is 3 years old and plays soccer in Winter Park, Florida.

Last weekend his i9 Sports league told his parents he could not play anymore. The league said other parents complained he was pushing kids. His mama, Martasia Griffin, says the real problem was that he kept scoring.

"They're mad at my son because he actually plays soccer," she said.

His parents posted the video. It got millions of views in days, and a GoFundMe for his training passed $50K.

Eddie Johnson saw it too. He played for the U.S. national team and runs the EJE7 Soccer Academy in Orlando. Nevada starts training with him in January.

"We should be praising a three-year-old with his ability instead of tarnishing it," Johnson said.
