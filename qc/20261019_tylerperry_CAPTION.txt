After his mama Willie Maxine passed in 2009, Tyler Perry sent a letter to every relative who was living off his support.

"Listen, you've got 60 days to become gainfully employed because I'm not going to keep supporting you like this."

They all got jobs. Not all big jobs, he said, but jobs they could take pride in.

One of his aunts kept asking for money, so he gave her a job instead. She stopped showing up and stopped calling in. He let her go.

On Kirk Franklin's Den of Kings, he explained it like this.

"I want to help you build, not be welfare to you."
