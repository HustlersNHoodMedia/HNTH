Before dawn on October 18, 2017, FBI agents broke down the front door of a home outside Atlanta and set off a flash grenade in the living room. Trina Martin and her boyfriend at the time, Toi Cliatt, hid in the bedroom closet. Her 7-year-old son hid under his bed, screaming for his mama. Toi ended up in handcuffs.

They had the wrong house. The man the FBI was after lived on a different street. The agent who led the team said his GPS sent him there, then threw the GPS away.

The family took it all the way to the Supreme Court and won 9-0 last year. This week an appeals court ruled they still can't sue the government. They can only go after the agent, and their lawyers are asking the full court to hear it again.

Trina had to stop coaching track because the starting pistol brought that morning back. Her son said it plainly when he was 13: "I didn't really have a childhood."
