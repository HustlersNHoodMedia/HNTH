A pipe broke under Brenda Daurham's kitchen sink on Valentine's Day and flooded the house she has lived in for more than 40 years. The Markham fire department came out. She called Allstate and says they told her she was covered and not to worry about a thing.

Excel Fire and Water Damage Restoration dried the house out. The bill came to $17,827.96. Then Allstate denied the claim, writing that the damage was wear and tear, deterioration and seepage. Excel wrote back that everything they saw pointed to a sudden plumbing failure, and asked Allstate to show what it based that decision on. Allstate has not.

On August 20 the restoration company filed a lien for that amount against her house with the Cook County Clerk. She is 81. She has been out of the home for six months and it still has no running water.

People started tagging Tyler Perry under the local news story. He paid the lien this week and said he would cover the repairs too.

Allstate still has not paid her claim.
