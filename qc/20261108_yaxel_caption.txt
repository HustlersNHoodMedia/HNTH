Yaxel Lendeborg handed his mama the keys to a house this week. To understand why it hit the way it did, you have to go back to Pennsauken, New Jersey.

In high school his grades kept him off the basketball team season after season. He only got to play the last 11 games of his senior year.

That's when Yissel Raposo took her son to work with her for a day. "I said, 'That's what you want for your life?' He said no. I said, 'That's why you need to play basketball.'"

He went the long way after that. Junior college. Then UAB. Then Michigan, where he won a national championship in April with her in the stands. This summer the Warriors drafted him, and he's already starting for them.

The house came out of his first NBA money. He filmed the surprise and put it on his YouTube.

"This the ultimate goal as a son," he said.
