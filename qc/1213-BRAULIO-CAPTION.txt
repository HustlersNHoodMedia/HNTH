Braulio is 87 and he still pushes an ice cream cart through Pilsen every day in the heat. He is from Iguala, Guerrero. He has never asked anybody for anything except the chance to keep working.

A man named Ventura Orozco Osorio ran into him by chance on the sidewalk, talked to him and posted the video. Millions of people watched it and the comments all said the same thing: let this man rest.

Then Ventura went back to find him. It took him almost an hour and a half of walking the neighborhood before he spotted Braulio sitting alone in the park. He showed him the video on his phone and told him that strangers all over the country wanted to help him retire.

"He never asked for fame or charity. He only ever asked for the chance to work. But now he deserves rest, dignity and peace."

The fundraiser is up and every dollar goes to Braulio.
