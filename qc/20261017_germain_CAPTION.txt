Esclanette Germain is a single mom working at FedEx and going to school to become a community health worker. For two years she did it with no car. Every day she took her little one to daycare on buses and trains, went to work, and rode back.

Then a vocational high school south of Boston got a 2009 Acura MDX through Second Chance Cars. It came in rough. About ten students in the auto and collision programs spent three months on it. Brakes, suspension, axles, tires, lights. One of them welded a patch over the rust.

On Wednesday they handed her the keys and she drove it out of the shop.

"I think having this car will make a big difference in my life," she said.
