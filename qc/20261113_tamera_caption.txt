Tamera Mowry was a teenager when Sister, Sister made her a household name. This Sunday her daughter Ariah, 11, makes her own acting debut in Tamera's new Hallmark movie, Mistletoe and Mimosas, playing a younger version of her mom's character.

Ariah had been asking to be in one of her mom's movies for over a year. Tamera said yes with one condition: she had to audition for the role like everybody else. She booked it.

"I was proud of her because she was very independent," Tamera said. Watching her daughter's first scene, she was "literally crying."

Ariah already booked her next role too, a school play. Her mom will be working backstage for that one.
