Johnnie "DJ" Jones was the top player Missouri signed out of high school this year, an 18-year-old offensive lineman from Florida. He never got to play a college game.

This summer, during the routine physical every freshman takes, the team's medical staff caught a problem with his heart. When it didn't get better, they sent him to the Mayo Clinic in Jacksonville, and the doctors there found something much more serious. Last week the team found out he needed a heart transplant.

On Saturday he watched his teammates beat Florida from the hospital. On Tuesday his coach, Eli Drinkwitz, asked fans to pray for him because "he needs help in a hurry."

Wednesday night, DJ got his new heart. The surgery went well, and he's recovering.
