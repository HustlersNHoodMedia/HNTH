A man joined a Facebook group called "Black JEEP Owners" because his Jeep is black. 🚙

Cory Allen posted his photos anyway. Two shots of his Wrangler, one caption: I totally misunderstood the group name but I'm just gonna roll with it.

The group has 46,500 members. The admins could have removed him in one click.

Nobody did. 🤝

65K reactions. 2,300 comments. 4,100 shares. And when he came back to say something, it wasn't an apology:

"I didn't expect this to blow up like this but y'all are awesome and thanks for the invite to the cookout" 🔥

Then the comments turned into something else. People started telling on themselves.

A guy who joined a black dog owner group in the late 90s and showed up at a park confused about why most of the dogs weren't black. Nobody cared, everybody laughed, his dog got treats and belly rubs. So did he. 🐕

A woman from Tennessee who joined an all black roast group out of Maryland by mistake. She says she sounds like corn bread so they could barely understand her. She joined on Christmas and somebody posted "Santa sent us a snowflake for Christmas." She was in. ❄️

A 16-year-old who walked into a black crochet group full of grandmas carrying all her black plushies. They wouldn't let her leave. They fed her. 🧶

And a woman who saw an African American biker club in her town and asked if she could join without a bike. Her inbox flooded. She still goes.

Every one of them the same story. Walked in by accident. Got adopted on purpose.
