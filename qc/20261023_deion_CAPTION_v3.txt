Deion Sanders was raised by his mama, Connie Knight, in Fort Myers, Florida. She cleaned hospitals and worked night shifts to keep the family going.

"My mom never saw me play a football, baseball or basketball game in high school because she was working," he said. "She worked for me."

As a kid he promised her that one day she'd never have to work again.

In 1989 the Atlanta Falcons took him with the fifth pick. The day he signed, he kept that promise and retired his mama for good.

The woman who couldn't make it to his high school games walked around Colorado in 2023 with her own football access pass, holding her son's hand.
