Keyana and her four kids were homeless in Georgia. She called it a leap of faith: she put the kids on a bus with the last little bit of money she had and started over in a new city, where all they have right now is a couch.

A stranger walked up and asked if she had a dollar or anything, because he was trying to find some food. "I just got a couple of dollars," she said, and gave it to him anyway.

The stranger was Jimmy Darts. He handed her $500, and she broke down while her daughter held on to her.

Her dream is to go to trade school for HVAC so her kids never have to worry about losing a home again.

Jimmy started a fundraiser to get them into a permanent home. In about three hours, more than 1,000 people had sent over $26K.

"We literally came here with the clothes on our backs."
