Kountry Wayne has 10 kids, and he just said something a lot of fathers are not going to like.

He told TSR he helps pay the bills in the homes where his kids live, even when their mama has moved on and another man is in the house. His reason is that the job she's doing every day counts. Taking them to school, sitting with them when they're sick, sitting through the games.

"It's easy to look out for the household that you in. You don't get no credit for that."

He said a lot of guys hear from their mama or their new girl that they're doing too much for the mother of their kids. His answer is that no woman he talks to is going to stop him from looking out for them.

"Your baby mama ain't your blesser. God's your blesser."

Ten kids. Is he right?
