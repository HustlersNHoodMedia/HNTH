Corion Evans was 16, sitting in his car by the I-10 boat launch in Moss Point, Mississippi, at 2:30 in the morning when he watched a car drive straight down the ramp into the Pascagoula River. Three girls inside. The driver said later her GPS told her to keep going.

The car floated about twenty feet out and started going under.

"I was just like, I can't let none of these folks die. They need to get out the water."

He threw his shoes, his shirt and his phone on the ground and went in. His boy KJ went in behind him. They got the girls up on the roof of the car, then started swimming them back one at a time.

Officer Gary Mercer showed up and jumped in too. One of the girls panicked, grabbed him and pulled him under.

"I turned around, I see the police officer. He's drowning. He's going underwater saying help. So I went over there and grabbed him and swam him back until I felt myself I could walk."

Everybody made it out. The city gave him a certificate. His mama said she was proud he wasn't thinking about himself.

He'd been swimming since he was three.
