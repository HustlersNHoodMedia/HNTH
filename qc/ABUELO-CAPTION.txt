A man held his bag up to the side of the ice machine and waited.

He had the clear bag open in both hands, pressed flat against the panel, eyes locked on it, ready for the ice to come down out of the front like a soda cup.

It was already out. A full bag of it had dropped into the metal chute by his foot and sat there the whole time he was standing up there holding that empty bag open.

Anybody who has ever walked up to one of those machines knows exactly how he ended up there.
