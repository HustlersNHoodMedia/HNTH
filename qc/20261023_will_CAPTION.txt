Will Smith's father ran a refrigeration shop in Philly. One day he decided the shop needed a new wall, and he put Will and his brother Harry on the job.

Every day after school they dug the foundation and mixed the concrete by hand, one brick at a time.

Will looked at that hole and thought it would never be anything but a hole. It took them a year and a half.

When it was done, their father stood there with them and said: "Don't y'all never tell me you can't do something."
