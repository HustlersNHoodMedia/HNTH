Gigi is 92 and she lives in Franklin Park, New Jersey. One afternoon she walked out of her house and nobody in there knew it. Her own doorbell camera caught her going down the driveway until she was out of the frame.

A few streets over, Wilmar was working his Amazon route when she waved at him from the sidewalk.

"People wave at you all the time as an Amazon driver. But she was waving at me like something happened."

So he pulled over and went to check. That's when he saw the band on her wrist with her name on it and who to call. He called her granddaughter Karen, who was out taking her own mother to the doctor, got the address from her, and put Gigi in the van.

The same camera that filmed her walking away filmed him bringing her back. Opening the door for her. Taking her hand. Walking her all the way up to the house.

He finished his route after.
