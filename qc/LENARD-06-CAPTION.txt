A year. Same road, same time, same young man walking to work.

Lenard walks about four miles to his job at FedEx in North Charleston. Leaves around 8:30 in the morning. Heat, rain, doesn't matter. He gets there soaked in sweat and he gets there on time.

A man had been passing him on that road for a year. He assumed the kid was walking to school. Then he found out he was walking to a job.

Two weeks ago he pulled over and handed him a brand new electric scooter. Bought a spare battery to go with it so he'd never end up stranded halfway. Then stood on the side of the road until Lenard knew how to ride it.

Lenard took off down the same road he'd been walking for a year.

He never asked anybody for a ride. He just kept showing up.
