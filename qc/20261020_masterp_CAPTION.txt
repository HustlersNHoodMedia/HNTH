Master P was selling records out of his own store in Richmond, California when Jimmy Iovine handed him a check for $1 million.

Then he read the contract. For seven years he couldn't use his own name, and the label would take his rights.

"Sir, I'm going to get something to eat and I'mma come back," he told him. He never came back.

He kept building No Limit on his own and held on to his masters. In 1998 Forbes ranked him No. 10 among the highest-paid entertainers, with an estimated $56.5 million.

"You gotta know your self-worth."
