Courtney Williams got on a stream with her friends and said it plain. "I just bought my daddy a house." Then she told them she bought her mama one too.

If you watch the Minnesota Lynx you already know her dad, Don. He is the man in the stands in her #10 jersey, on his feet all game. He has been at her games since rec league, through middle school, high school and college. During the season he takes out the trash and takes care of the dogs so all she has to think about is basketball.

"I love seeing him up there turnt up," she said. Before this season started somebody asked her about him. "Oh man, that's my number one hype."

He put it his own way. "Remember who she is, because I'm nobody without her."

Last season Courtney made $180,000. This year the WNBA players held the line and won a new deal, and she signed with the Lynx for $2.4 million over two years. She used it to take care of the two people who got her there.
