Marlon Jackson is 69, and this week he said out loud what he had been carrying for four years: prostate cancer, now stage 4. He revealed it on a podcast.

He never came off the road. After Tito passed in 2024, Marlon and his brother Jackie kept the Jacksons touring, singing the songs they made with Michael. In June he took a hard fall on stage in Wichita during "Wanna Be Startin' Somethin'," and fans had no idea what he was going through.

He has had his prostate removed and done radiation. He walks almost four miles, four times a week, cut out sugar, and says even his doctor is surprised by how well he's doing.

"I'm great. I'm good. Bad grass don't die," he said. "So I'm going to be around for a long time."
