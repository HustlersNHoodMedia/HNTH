Peace Prophet sells her paintings on the sidewalk in downtown Chicago. The small ones go for $5. She's 71, and she has been living on the street since 2000.

Before that she was Janice Carol Rivers. She graduated from DePaul in 1978 with a degree in sociology and did social work at the Ida B. Wells projects. Her daughter says she helped write the grant that got the kids there a new playground.

She paints every day, right there on the street. "I'm doing this art thing, hustling around," she said. One collector has bought close to 1,000 of her pieces since 2012, and her work is in a museum collection.

Another collector, Geri Biamonte, made a video about her paintings and put it on YouTube in 2019. Earlier this year that video led her children to her. On her 71st birthday her daughter called on FaceTime and her granddaughter sang to her. "It's the best birthday I ever had," she said.
