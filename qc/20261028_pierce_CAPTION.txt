Paul Pierce made about $195 million over 19 seasons in the NBA, and now he says he's drowning financially.

In new filings in his child support case, Pierce says his income dropped hard after he lost his job at Fox Sports last year. Before that he was bringing in about $119K a month. Right now he pays around $28K a month in child support and about $20K in spousal support, and after that he says he's left with $3,793 a month.

The Truth has four kids, including a son born in February.

His lawyer put it plain: Pierce is "tapped out."
