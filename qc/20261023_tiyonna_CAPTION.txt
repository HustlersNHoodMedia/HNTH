Tiyonna Betts was out in Cleveland when she saw a little girl in a pink jacket crying on the side of a busy road, all by herself.

The girl was 6. Her school bus had dropped her off almost 40 minutes early, and nobody was there to meet her.

Tiyonna stopped and got her in the car. The little girl knew the landmarks on the way home, so she pointed and Tiyonna drove until they pulled up at her house.

"God led me to that baby," Tiyonna said.

Her mama, Mari Richardson, got her daughter back safe and hugged the woman who brought her home. The bus company says the driver was disciplined.
