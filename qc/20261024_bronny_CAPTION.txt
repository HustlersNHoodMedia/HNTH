In the summer of 2023, Bronny James went into cardiac arrest during a practice at USC. He was 18.

15 months later, on opening night in Los Angeles, he checked into an NBA game right next to his dad. LeBron and Bronny became the first father and son to ever play together in the league.

LeBron said standing at the scorer's table with his son "is something I will never forget."
