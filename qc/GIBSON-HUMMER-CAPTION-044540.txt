Antonio Gibson met his boss at a hiring event at his high school in Gray, Georgia. He started at Slim Chickens and the first stretch was rough. He kept showing up anyway, and he turned into the one in the kitchen everybody leans on.

Then his first real check hit. More than $1,000. His big boy check.

He took part of it and bought food, snacks and Red Bulls for the whole crew.

What he didn't know was that the crew knew he'd been looking for a car he could afford. The owner, Justin Kelly, talked it over with everybody and they couldn't find a reason not to.

So they called him out front, handed him the keys to a Hummer and put the title in his name.

It's his.
