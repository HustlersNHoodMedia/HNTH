Bobby Scott started working for the Braswell family farm in Nashville, North Carolina when he was 17. He stayed for 63 years. The farm passed through four generations of the same family, and Bobby was there for all of them.

Last month, at 85, he finally retired. They threw him a party and handed him a plaque that says 63 years of service. His son Martez says he's finally going to take a few vacations with the family.

"I've enjoyed the work, and I have always tried to do my best at what I did," Bobby said.
