She was an analyst on Wall Street who started making lipstick in her Brooklyn kitchen, because nothing on the shelf was made for her.

Shark Tank told her no in 2015. She built it anyway. The Lip Bar ended up in more than 2,000 stores, and she put up billboards in four cities clowning the show for passing on her.

Her second line, Thread Beauty, went into Target in 2022. Everything $8. It never caught the same way, and last year she thought about shutting it down. Target told her to keep going.

So instead of killing it she got on camera and said it plain, then made an offer: come be the face of Thread and own part of it. No money up front. Just equity. The form is on her site.

And this is the same woman who watched the Target boycott over the DEI rollback take 30 to 40% of her sales in there, said the number out loud, and still told folks not to go back.
