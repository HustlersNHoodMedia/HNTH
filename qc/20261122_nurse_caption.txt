Andrea Small is a nurse and case manager at a hospital in Orange City, Florida. In September of last year, a baby boy named Westin was born there, and he couldn't leave the hospital with his parents.

His parents spent about three hours calling relatives and friends who could take him for a while. Nobody could.

So Andrea offered, and both parents agreed. Her coworkers showed up with a pack and play, formula and everything else he needed, and when her shift ended at 11 p.m. she took him home. He was 2 days old.

Andrea knows what it is to lose a baby. Her first daughter, Madelynn, was born early and lived six and a half weeks.

Last month she officially adopted Westin. He's 1 now, and she still keeps his birth mom and grandma in his life.

"I didn't get to make him, but I'm shaping him as a person."
