Warrick Dunn's mother, Betty Smothers, was a police officer in Baton Rouge raising six kids by herself. She worked a second job at night and still never made enough to buy a house. She passed in January 1993, two days after his 18th birthday, and he finished raising his brothers and sisters.

In 1997, his first year in the NFL, he started helping single parents with the down payment on their first home and filling it with furniture and groceries. More than 250 families later, he was back at it this week in Florida.

"Every home we present, I'm reminded of my mom's work ethic," he said.
