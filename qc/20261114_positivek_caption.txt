Positive K is spending this season of his life taking care of his mother. Julie has been living with Alzheimer's for years, and her son, Darryl Gibson, is her caregiver. He picks out her outfits. He watches her movement and her speech change. He says there have been hard days, the kind caregivers don't post about, and that he had to learn to ask his family for help, because nobody carries this alone.

This is the same man who gave rap one of its biggest records in 1992. "I Got a Man" hit the Billboard top 15, and people still quote it thirty years later. He says he's grown more as a son through this than he ever did in a studio, and he still plans to make music again someday. But right now he's exactly where he's supposed to be.

"This is my assignment in life."
