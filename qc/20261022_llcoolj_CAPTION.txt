In 1989, LL Cool J dropped Walking With a Panther and the critics went at him. Hip-hop was getting harder, and they said he had gone too soft and too commercial. A lot of folks thought his run was over.

It got to him. "I was beefing, moaning, and griping about what people were saying," he said later.

So he took it to his grandmother. She told him to get out there and knock 'em out.

He went back in the studio and made her words the title of his next album, with her as the mama. "Mama Said Knock You Out" barely got played on MTV or the radio at first.

It went double platinum anyway, and in 1992 it won him his first Grammy over MC Hammer, Ice-T and Queen Latifah.
