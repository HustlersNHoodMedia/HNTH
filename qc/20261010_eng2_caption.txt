Frederick Engram Jr. has played football since he was 3 years old. In April, he was outside at school playing with his friends when something didn't feel right. He thought it was an asthma attack. The school nurse said it wasn't.

It was a stroke in his spinal cord. He was 12. "My whole right leg was paralyzed. I cannot walk. I couldn't do nothing. Can't move my leg or nothing," he said. Doctors told the family he'd be in a walker or a wheelchair for six months to a year.

It took him about a month to walk again. "It was like one thing he couldn't do that one day, the next day he was able to do it," his mama Trisha said.

He's playing flag football while he gets back to 100%, and she believes he'll be back in pads with his team next year.

"God really put me in the situation for a reason," Frederick said. "He put me in the situation to never give up."
