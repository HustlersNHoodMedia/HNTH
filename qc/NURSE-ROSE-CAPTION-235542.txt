Diamond Rose is an ER nurse at Memorial Hermann in Houston. Early Sunday morning she and her friends walked out of a club on West Gray and somebody started shooting over an argument that had nothing to do with them.

She thought it was fireworks. Then her legs started stinging. When she got somewhere safe she saw the blood, took off her pants and tied them around her leg to stop it.

She told the ambulance to take her to her own hospital. She always said she never wanted to come in as a level one trauma in front of her coworkers. That's exactly what happened. "But they were great, they were really great."

Three bullets. They took two out of her knees. The one in her thigh is staying, too dangerous to remove. She's walking with a walker now.

She has a little boy at home. She says thinking about him is what got her through.

"That is why I wanted to make sure I was okay, so I could survive and be here for him."

Nobody has been arrested.
