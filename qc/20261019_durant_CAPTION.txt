Wanda Durant had her first son at 18. By 21 she was a single mom raising two boys in Prince George's County, Maryland.

On May 6, 2014, her youngest accepted the NBA MVP in Oklahoma City. He spent most of the speech thanking his teammates one by one, his coaches, his brother. Then he turned to her in the front row.

"The odds were stacked against us," he said. "You kept us off the street."

"You went to sleep hungry. You sacrificed for us. You the real MVP."
