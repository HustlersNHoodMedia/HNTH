Samuel Monroe Jr. was in Menace II Society, Tales From the Hood, Set It Off, Don't Be a Menace and The Players Club. If you grew up on those movies, you know his face.

He caught meningitis working a job in Las Vegas. His family says several hospitals misdiagnosed him before it reached his brain and his spine. What came after was nine months across multiple hospitals and two rehab centers, MRSA pneumonia, kidney failure, dialysis, brain surgeries.

He passed Wednesday night at a Los Angeles hospital. He was 52. His mother, Joyce Patton, asked people to keep praying for his three children.

Rest easy.
