Sunday evening at Rockaway Beach, past swimming hours, two friends got pulled into a rip current near Beach 84th.

When the lifeguards reached them, Sir Audley Sykes sent them the other way. His mother says he told them to go get his best friend out of the water first.

They pulled both of them out. Only one of them came home.

Sir was 17. He was about to start his senior year at Williamsburg High School. He played quarterback and front line for the Rosedale Jets. He was the oldest of seven brothers, and a few days before, he had left a birthday gift on his mother's bed.

"He would save someone else before he thought about himself," Patrice Thompson said. "That's the thing I appreciated about him the most."

She's raising money for the funeral and for counseling for his six little brothers.

Sources: ABC7 New York, QNS, GoFundMe organized by Patrice Thompson.
