One of those houses is not for any of them. It is for the Clover Boyz, the friends Cenat had before the cameras. Dezz, Ray, Punga, Reggie, Rakai, Tota, Tylil.

That is the whole thing. Anybody who gets money can buy a big house somewhere quiet. Buying the block and moving the day ones onto it is a different decision.
