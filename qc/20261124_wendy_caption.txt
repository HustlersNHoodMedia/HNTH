Wendy Williams wanted to spend Thanksgiving with her family in Miami. Her lawyer, Joe Tacopina, says her court-appointed guardian told her no.

"She wants to spend Thanksgiving with her family in Miami. That's all she wants to do, be with her family," he said in a radio interview. He says Wendy even offered to travel with the security she is already required to use. He also says the guardian holds all of her IDs, so she can't just go anywhere.

Wendy has been under guardianship since 2022 and lives in a care facility in New York. In 2024 her care team said she had been diagnosed with frontotemporal dementia. The guardian's lawyers have said she is free to see her family and has visited Florida before.

Her team is still fighting to end the guardianship. A court date set for November 6 was canceled, with no new one on the calendar, and Thanksgiving is Thursday.
