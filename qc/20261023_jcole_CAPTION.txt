J. Cole was a kid when his mom Kay moved him and his brother out of a trailer park and into a house on Forest Hills Drive in Fayetteville, North Carolina. She worked at the post office. "We were able to get the house, because there was another income," he said.

That's the house he started rapping in.

After he left for college in New York, his mom's marriage fell apart and she couldn't keep up with the payments. They lost the house.

In 2014, Cole found out it was back on the market and bought it back. "I never liked the way that they took the house from us."

He named his next album after the address. 2014 Forest Hills Drive went platinum with no features.
