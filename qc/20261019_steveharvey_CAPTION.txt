Back in the late '60s in Cleveland, Steve Harvey's sixth grade teacher asked the class to write down what they wanted to be when they grew up. Steve had a bad stutter. He wrote "I want to be on TV."

She saved his paper for last, called him up in front of everybody and asked how somebody who could barely talk was going to be on television. Then she called his parents.

His mama was upset. His daddy asked one question: "Well, what's wrong with that?" He told Steve to keep that paper and read it every morning and every night.

Years later, Steve was on TV every day. And every Christmas while that teacher was alive, he sent her a television.

"I wanted her to see me."
