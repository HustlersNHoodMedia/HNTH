T-Boz's daughter Chase Rolison sat down on Reginae Carter's podcast this week and told what really happened to her last year. Lupus put her in the hospital for almost a month. She nearly lost her life three separate times. Her memory was so affected she thought she'd only been there three days. She had to learn how to sit up, how to eat, how to walk all over again.

Her mama knows that fight better than anybody. Tionne was diagnosed with sickle cell as a little girl, and doctors told her family she wouldn't live past 30 or ever have kids. She's 56 now, she filled stages with TLC for three decades, and just last month she was pushing through another sickle cell crisis of her own while Chase was getting back on her feet.

The daughter they said would never come is here, walking, telling her own story.
