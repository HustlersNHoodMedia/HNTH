Prince put out 39 studio albums while he was alive. At 19 he played all 27 instruments on his first one by himself.

He wrote "Nothing Compares 2 U," "Manic Monday" and "I Feel For You" and gave them to other artists. And that was just what he let go of.

Everything else went into the vault at Paisley Park. Susan Rogers, who engineered for him in the '80s, said the vault was already there before Purple Rain. "When I left in '87, it was nearly full."

People close to the estate say there could be as many as 8,000 unreleased songs in there, enough to drop an album a year for the next 100 years. This summer they released Timeless, 10 songs nobody had heard, recorded between 1977 and 2016.
