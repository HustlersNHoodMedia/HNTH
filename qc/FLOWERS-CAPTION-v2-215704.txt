Samuel Monteiro drives a school bus in Brazil. Every kid on his route has their own handshake with him. He made them up, one for each child, so every single one of them starts the day feeling like somebody sees them.

One morning they flipped it on him.

The first kid got on and handed him a yellow flower. Then the next one. Then the next. Every child who climbed those steps had a flower for the driver.

He tried to keep it together. He couldn't. By the end he was sitting at the wheel with his hands full of flowers, wiping his eyes before he could pull off.

He posted it himself with one line: "Yes, a man can also receive flowers."

The kids still bring him flowers some mornings.
