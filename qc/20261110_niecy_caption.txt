Niecy Nash-Betts told Oprah she found out late that her father had fallen in Las Vegas. By the time anyone called her, he had gone from the hospital to a rehab center.

She flew out to pick up his passport and bank statements from the apartment he shares with his twin brother. When she walked in, her uncle was in even worse shape than her dad. "So now we taking two of y'all," she said.

Other relatives told her they didn't want anything to do with it. So she arranged transport for both men, brought them back, and took on their care. She said good care is expensive, and she wishes the world had more grace for people who step in.

Oprah asked what it's like to take care of somebody who wasn't there for you. Niecy didn't sugarcoat it.

"Not a school jacket, not a pair of shoes, not a nothing. And now everything that you need, I have to supply."

She said she stopped replaying what should have been and decided to move forward.
