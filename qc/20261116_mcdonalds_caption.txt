Patricia Williams and her husband cashed in their savings to get into the McDonald's franchise program in the early 1980s, while she was raising two young daughters.

When the marriage ended, she went to the bank for a loan to buy out her ex-husband's share. The banker told her to bring her husband in to talk terms. She wouldn't do it. Her name wasn't even on the original franchise agreement.

She kept building anyway, one restaurant at a time. Today her daughters, Nicole Enearu and Kerri Harper-Howie, run 18 McDonald's across Los Angeles County with about 1,100 employees.

"She came from such humble roots and she built an empire."
