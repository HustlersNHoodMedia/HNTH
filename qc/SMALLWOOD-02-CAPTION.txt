Wendell Smallwood Jr. could be out this week, and it has nothing to do with football.

His 8-year-old daughter Mila was diagnosed with cancer this year. Doctors have told the family she may have days or weeks.

On Friday, Aug. 7, a Gloucester County judge withdrew Smallwood's guilty plea in a New Jersey gun case and pointed straight at Mila's illness as the reason. A July appeals ruling on the state's assault weapons ban opened the door, and his record inside — the programs, the conduct — held it open.

He's 32. Drafted by the Eagles out of West Virginia in 2016, later with Washington and Pittsburgh. Since 2024 he's been serving 18 months on federal fraud charges tied to COVID relief money, with a state sentence running alongside it.

His lawyer put it plainer than any filing: "It's about whether his daughter will hold her dad again."

He could walk out as early as Monday.
