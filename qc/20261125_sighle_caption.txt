Her name is Sighle Williams. She started at Mac's Drive-Thru in Florida in 1987 and worked that window for 37 years. She knew her customers by name, and then she knew their kids.

What most of them didn't know: her sister and her niece couldn't take care of their children, so she raised all five girls as her own.

When she retired in 2024, the owner said every dollar that came through that day was hers. Cars wrapped around the block. One customer waited almost three hours.

"A lot of people don't know my story," she said.

Her last day is now a short film on Netflix, One Last Order.
