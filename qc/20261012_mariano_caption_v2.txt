Mariano Thomas was a 9 year old fourth grader when he felt a tingle on his way to school one Friday morning in 2010. Within 10 hours he couldn't move anything from the neck down.

His own immune system had attacked his spinal cord. He spent weeks paralyzed at Texas Children's before his movement slowly came back.

He went on to graduate with honors in neuroscience from Johns Hopkins. Today he's a third year med student at Meharry training to be a children's neurologist, and one day he wants to work at the hospital that saved him.

"If you would've told the 9 year old me laying in a hospital bed completely paralyzed that he'd be graduating with honors from Johns Hopkins, he'd believe you. I been HIM since birth."
