Freddie Wiggins has been cleaning floors at a hospital in Nashville since 1980.

Early one morning he walked past a woman crying in the hallway. He stopped and asked her what was wrong. Sarah Hart told him her mom had Lewy body dementia, and that morning she had lost the ability to speak.

Freddie gave her a hug. He said he knew he had to do something. When he got off work, he went upstairs looking for her mom.

He held her hand and started singing "My Girl." Her mom locked eyes with him. Then she started singing too. The nurses in the room were crying.

Sarah later asked Freddie to sing at her mom's funeral.

When the two of them sat down to talk about that morning, he told her why he stopped: "My mom had dementia too, and she always put us five kids first. So it's now time for me to give back."

Sarah's answer: "You have been an angel in my life."
