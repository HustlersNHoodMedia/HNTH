Devon Alexander grew up in Hyde Park, on the north side of St. Louis, one of 13 kids in a two bedroom house. He went to school with holes in his shoes. At 8 he walked into a boxing gym a former cop had set up in the basement of an old police station, and he kept walking there every day, asking himself why he never got shot on the way.

Thirty kids started in that basement. Nine of them are gone. About nine more went to prison. Devon went 300 and 10 as an amateur and won world titles in two weight classes.

He is 39 now, and for the past three years he has been paying out of his own pocket to run boxing for St. Louis kids through his foundation. No sparring for the little ones, just the footwork, the bag and the discipline. One mother says the bullying on her 8 year old stopped once she started training there.

Somebody asked him why he does it. How can you call yourself a champion and are never around the people? You got to give it all back.
