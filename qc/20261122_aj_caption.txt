AJ Enfield is 12 and plays defensive tackle on his seventh-grade football team in Minnesota. A few months ago his mom, Jodee, was driving when her car hit a dump truck. Broken ribs, a smashed femur, surgery, and then the bills started coming.

She told him not to worry about it. He set up an auction anyway.

AJ put up his LEGO sets, his Hot Wheels collection, his Star Wars figures, his board games and his movies. He even auctioned himself off to cook somebody brunch. He raised just under $800, and his team raised another $1,000. His mom says it got them caught up on every bill.

Some of the buyers gave the toys back. AJ wants to take them to a shelter before Christmas so other kids have presents to open.

"Family takes care of each other and I just really care about my mom."
