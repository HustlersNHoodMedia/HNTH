Beyoncé grew up around her mama's hair salon in Houston, where Tina Knowles mixed her own treatments for her clients. That's where Cécred started.

When it came time to raise money, investors looked at the budget and didn't like it. Most of the money was going to research and testing. They wanted it moved to marketing.

Beyoncé said no and paid for the whole company herself.

"She decided, 'I'm just gonna fund it myself,' which is crazy expensive," Tina said this month. She admitted she wasn't sure her daughter knew what she was signing up for.

The formulas took six years. When Cécred hit Ulta in 2025, it became the store's most successful high-end haircare launch ever.
