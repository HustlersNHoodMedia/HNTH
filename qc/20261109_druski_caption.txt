For years Netflix kept passing on Druski's projects. FX passed on a show, Amazon passed on a movie. So he stopped pitching and started paying for everything himself, millions of his own money into Coulda Been Love, Coulda Been Daddy, Coulda Been Records.

Last week Netflix signed him to a multi-year deal, and those same shows he paid for out of his own pocket are going on Netflix. Five days later he walked out on that SNL stage as the first internet creator to ever host the show.

He told everybody back in September: "I'm just betting on myself."

In the monologue he looked at his family in the crowd and asked how he could ever pay them back. His grandma didn't miss a beat: "Some money."
