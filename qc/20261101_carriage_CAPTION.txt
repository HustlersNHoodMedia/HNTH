A dad dressed his little girls up as princesses for Halloween, then hooked a princess carriage to the back of his bike and pedaled them around the neighborhood to trick-or-treat.

The carriage had a tall white canopy, fairy lights all over it and big white wheels. The girls rode in the back while he did all the pedaling up front.

At one house a neighbor walked out to the street and handed the candy right into the carriage, so the princesses never had to climb out.

A neighbor caught it on video and it's been shared all over since Halloween night. One comment summed it up: "That's a dad."
