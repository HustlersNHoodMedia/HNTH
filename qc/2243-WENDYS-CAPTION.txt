A manager at a Wendy's in Murfreesboro, Tennessee tied a neurodivergent employee to the grill with trash bags.

According to the police report, manager Michiko Burton did it because she needed him to "stay put" while she worked in the back of the restaurant. A video recorded inside the store shows him standing there bound to the equipment.

Burton told police it was a TikTok trend and that it was meant as a joke. His co-worker Nicholus Murphy was not buying it: "If there was a TikTok trend, then where's the TikTok?"

Officers interviewed the employee, who said he was not upset and was not hurt. No charges were filed.

A Wendy's area manager called it unacceptable and the general manager confirmed Burton was fired.

His family is now asking Murfreesboro police to reopen the investigation.
