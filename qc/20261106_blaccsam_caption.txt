Growing up, Nipsey Hussle and his big brother Blacc Sam lived with their mom and their grandma. Their dad came through on weekends, and when he did, he went all out: the beach, the toy store, whatever they wanted.

Then they wouldn't see him again for about three weeks.

As Sam got older, he figured out why. "Pop spending the whole check on us," he said. "And then you got to wait until he get that check again to see us again."

So he pulled his little brother to the side. When we see Pop, don't ask for nothing. Let's just go to the movies, or stay at the house and watch one.

Sam told the story sitting right next to him, their father Dawit, on the One54 podcast this week.
