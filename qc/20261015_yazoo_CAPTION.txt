Forty families in Yazoo City, Mississippi moved into brand new brick houses at Roberts Estates 19 years ago. The deal was rent now, buy later. When the time came, each family could buy their home for $15,000 out of pocket.

Then it got stuck. The price was so low that no bank would write a mortgage that small, and the sales wouldn't cover what the developer still owed. So the families kept paying rent.

And through all of it, nobody left. 38 of the 40 houses still belong to the same families that moved in, and the other two are their kids.

A community lender out of Jackson ended up buying the whole neighborhood and stacking grants and forgivable loans until the numbers worked. This week they celebrated becoming homeowners.

Dawn Dodd said it at the ceremony: "Over 15 years of wondering and over 15 years of dreaming about having a place to call our own. Today, look at God! We will be homeowners."
