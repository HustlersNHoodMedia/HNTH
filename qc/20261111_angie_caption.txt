In 1996, a few months before Tupac passed, Angie Martinez was a young radio host just getting started in New York. Tupac reached out, told her his problem was never with all of New York, and invited her to his home in Los Angeles to sit down and talk.

She aired the parts that carried his message, that he wasn't at war with a whole coast. The angrier, more personal parts she held back. With the East Coast vs. West Coast tension at its peak, she felt they could hurt people and make a dangerous situation worse.

After he passed, she felt she had to protect what was left on those tapes. "I felt a responsibility to protect something that was never meant to become his final statement."

Thirty years later, she says it's a piece of hip-hop history that shouldn't stay with her forever. The full interview comes out November 27 as The Tupac Tapes, a four-part video podcast on Netflix.
