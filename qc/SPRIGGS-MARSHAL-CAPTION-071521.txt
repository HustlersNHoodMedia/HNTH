Caroll H. Spriggs II is 53 and he has been in the fire service for 38 years. It started at the Huntingtown Volunteer Fire Department, the kind of station where nobody gets paid and you go because the tones drop.

From there: deputy fire chief in Annapolis, deputy fire chief in Prince George's County, task force officer with the ATF working explosives, elected to the National Bomb Squad Commanders Advisory Board in 2019 and named its secretary the year after.

This week Maryland State Police named him State Fire Marshal for a six year term. He runs the oldest state fire marshal's office in the country, 85 people including more than 42 deputy fire marshals, covering fire and arson investigations, explosives, code enforcement and prevention for the whole state.

He is the first Black man to hold that job in the office's 132 year history. He had already been doing it in an acting role since December 2023.

"I look forward to working with all of the partners to continue making Maryland safe."
