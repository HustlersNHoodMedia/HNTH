For 25 years, a chemistry teacher in New York named Anthony Simonelli made his students the same promise. Score a perfect 100 on the state chemistry exam and he would drive to your house and hand you a $100 bill. Thousands of kids took that test. Nobody collected.

This year JoJo Yawson did. He's 16. He was homeschooled for years, he tests with accommodations, and his was the last exam folder they graded. It was also the last year of that version of the test.

On June 26, Mr. Simonelli pulled up to JoJo's house with the bill. He had written "Thank you" and his name across it in marker. JoJo's mom wants to frame it, so when things get hard he can look at it and remember people are behind him.

JoJo had already scored 100 on the biology exam the year before. Ask him if the money mattered and he'll tell you straight. "Of course it's about the money," he said. "If they gave me $100 for getting a 50, I would have got a 50."
