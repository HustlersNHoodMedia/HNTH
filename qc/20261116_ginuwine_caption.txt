Ginuwine says he's out $4,200 for a cruise he never chose to skip.

He booked eight nights on Royal Caribbean's Legend of the Seas, November 14 to 22. Then the nationwide FAA ground stop hit, his flights got canceled twice, and he never made it to the ship.

He says he explained everything to Royal Caribbean and they refused him a refund or even a future cruise credit. "I did not voluntarily cancel my vacation. I was prevented from traveling due to circumstances beyond my control."

His point is that nobody can be expected to fly in two or three days early just in case of a national emergency. "People have jobs, responsibilities, and obligations." He says he's not asking for special treatment, "just fairness and consideration."

Royal Caribbean or Ginuwine, who's right on this one?
