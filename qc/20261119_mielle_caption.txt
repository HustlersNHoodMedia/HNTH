Monique Rodriguez spent nine years as a registered nurse. On the side, she mixed hair products at home and posted her recipes online, and women kept asking her how she grew her hair.

In 2014 she launched Mielle out of her garage with one product. She named the brand after her daughters, Mia and Mackenzie.

One product turned into more than 30, then shelves at Target, Walmart and Sally's. In 2023, Procter & Gamble bought the company.

Now her daughter Mia, 21, a marketing student at Hampton University, is joining Mielle as its first next-gen Creative and Culture Advisor.

"She is my legacy, and I know that she's going to carry the torch very well," Monique said.

Mia knows people will call her a nepo baby. She says she's fully aware of it, and she plans to earn the spot anyway.
