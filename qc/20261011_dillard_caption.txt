Danielle Dillard is raising four kids by herself in Southaven, Mississippi, right outside Memphis. For two years they have lived in the same rental in Colonial Hills.

Last week she got a letter from her landlord. She has until November 30 to get out.

She says her landlord sold the house to SpaceXAI, Elon Musk's company, which runs a data center in Southaven. Neighbors filed a class action lawsuit saying the place sounds like a jet engine all night. The company has been offering to buy homes nearby if the owners agree not to join that lawsuit.

Now she needs a four bedroom place she can afford in the same school zone so her kids don't have to switch schools. She has been approved for a new spot, but the move came out of nowhere and out of a budget that was already tight.

"Being a single mom, I live by a very strict budget in this economy that we live in."
