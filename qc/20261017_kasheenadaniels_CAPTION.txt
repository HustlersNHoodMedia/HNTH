Ka'Sheena Daniels was 31 and from New Orleans East. On September 25 she was out with friends on I-10 when they came up on a car flipped over near Elysian Fields. She recognized the driver. It was her neighbor.

"My friend KK jumped out," her friend Franchon Williams said.

KK pulled her neighbor out of the car. A few moments later another car slammed into one of the cars stopped on the interstate and pushed it into the people helping. KK went over the side.

Her mama got there and started screaming her name. "She said, 'Ma.' When they heard her say that, they ran where she was calling from." They got to her, but she didn't survive her injuries.

She worked in accounting and was raising her adopted son. She and Franchon were supposed to start paramedic school the next week. Franchon says she's still going, for her.

"If you needed help... and she could do it, it was done," her mother said.
