Romeo Miller was in Atlanta this past Sunday for the Insulin & Mimosas brunch, a day for families living with type 1 diabetes. For him it's personal.

His daughter River Rose was 18 months old when she started acting like she was in a daze. The family thought it was a bad flu. Her pediatrician checked her ketones and told them to get to the hospital immediately. Her body had stopped making insulin.

Since then it's been insulin before meals, constantly tracking her numbers, and nights at the hospital when it gets bad. Romeo has said the family hasn't taken a single day off since the diagnosis.

When he first shared it, he wrote: "Our bond is even stronger, because it's a must I be there for my daughter medically."
