Tedrick Washington Jr. is 23, from Blytheville, Arkansas. He played Division 1 basketball at Southeast Missouri State and Chattanooga.

On August 19 he was riding in the backseat of an Infiniti when the driver took off from Arkansas State Police. The chase hit 130 miles an hour before spike strips and a PIT maneuver ended it in a residential neighborhood. Washington wasn't driving.

Dashcam shows him in the backseat with both hands up. Trooper Theodore Henderson broke the rear window with his baton and told him to climb out through it. Washington says he couldn't get the door. Henderson grabbed his arm and his locs, pulled him through the opening, and punched him in the face. Then hit him again on the ground.

In his report, Henderson wrote that Washington "postured up."

The driver was charged with felony fleeing and DWI. Washington was charged with obstruction and resisting arrest. The other passenger in that same car was released without any charges at all.

Three weeks earlier, a different Arkansas trooper was recorded punching an 18-year-old girl in the face at a traffic stop in the same county. She's suing.

As of today, no discipline announced.
