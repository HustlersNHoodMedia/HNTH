Erick Hunter is from Capitol Heights, Maryland. He played five years at Morgan State and left second in school history in tackles.

His last season he started all 12 games and led the MEAC with 102 tackles. Fourteen for loss. Four sacks. Three forced fumbles. A pick. He blocked a field goal and ran it back 90 yards for a touchdown. Two-time MEAC Defensive Player of the Year. HBCU All-American.

The year before that he played two games. He was hurt for the rest of it.

Then the 2026 draft came and went and not one player who finished his career at an HBCU heard his name called. Not one, across seven rounds.

Detroit signed him off the open market a couple weeks later.

First preseason game, against Cincinnati, he played 43 defensive snaps and led the whole team in tackles. Dan Campbell has been saying his name since camp opened.

Nobody drafted him. He just kept doing on an NFL field what he was already doing at Morgan State.
