Debbie Allen is 76, and at the Ebony Power 100 gala this week she hit the dance floor with Kirk Franklin. The clip went everywhere.

Her son saw it too. Norman "Thump" Nixon Jr., a comedian, posted a video going off on Kirk, saying gospel music had gone too far and it was time to reel it in.

Then his mama pulled up under his post: "Thump please. Kirk meant no harm. You are being over protective."

He didn't back down. When somebody told him his mother has a life too, he wrote back that she does, and it's with his daddy. Debbie has been married to former Laker Norm Nixon since 1984.

It's not the first time. In 2022 she danced on stage with Patti LaBelle at Essence Fest, and she later said her son didn't speak to her for a week after that.

Thump or Debbie, who's right?
