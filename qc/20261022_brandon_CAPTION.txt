Brandon Bakke was adopted at birth and grew up in Fargo, North Dakota. In 2016, when he was 11, he started asking about his biological father. His mom Brandy went looking on Facebook and found his sister, Tiffany.

Tiffany told them their dad, Terrence, had passed the year before in Chicago at 56. He was buried in an unmarked grave because the family couldn't afford a stone.

Brandon spent the whole summer mowing lawns for neighbors and strangers and saved $400. When Dakota Monument heard what he was doing, they gave him the headstone for free and let him design it.

He put a heart on it, and two hands holding a bowl of soup, because Tiffany told him their dad used to feed the homeless.

"I don't think anybody should go unknown in life."
