Sha'Michael Russell was 13. On September 28 he was playing basketball with his siblings at his apartment complex in Shreveport when someone started shooting from a distance.

His aunt says he covered his little sister to keep the bullets off her. She made it. Three children were shot that night, and Sha'Michael was the one who didn't come home from the hospital.

His family remembers a boy who loved sports, his hair and Chinese food, who helped the neighbors and kept everybody laughing. Most of all he loved his baby sister.

A national youth foundation gave him its Youth Heroism Award. He is the first teenager to receive it after his death. Two 19-year-old cousins have been arrested and charged in the shooting.

On October 10 his family laid him to rest. The program read "Forever 13."

"He's a hero, he loved his baby sister," his cousin said.
