Kai was cleaning out her late grandmother's house when she found it in a drawer. A Rolleiflex from the 1960s, in almost perfect shape. She filmed every inch of it, then paid a camera repair shop in Los Angeles $450 to clean it and replace one mirror.

She says what came back wasn't the same camera. New scratches where there were none, dents, missing pieces and parts that don't match the videos she took before she dropped it off. The shop hasn't said anything publicly. She has filed a police report and is looking for a lawyer.

Three years before her grandmother passed in 2020, Kai filmed a documentary about her life. She grew up working the fields in Memphis as a sharecropper, went on to earn her degrees and ran her own business.

"It's more than just a camera to me. This is personal."
