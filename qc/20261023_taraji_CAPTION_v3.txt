Taraji P. Henson got pregnant while she was at Howard, so she became a mama before Hollywood ever knew her name.

After she graduated, she borrowed $700 from family and friends, packed up her son Marcell and moved to LA.

The early years were hard. She promised him she was never gonna give up, and she didn't. Baby Boy came, then Hustle & Flow, an Oscar nomination, Cookie on Empire and a star on the Hollywood Walk of Fame, with Marcell right next to her.

She carried so much of that struggle by herself that he grew up without feeling it.

"He don't even remember the hard days, he remembers the house with the pool," she said.
